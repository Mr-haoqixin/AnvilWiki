## Quest Overview

**Winter Practical** is one of the 44 quests in Songs of Glimmerwick.

| Property | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Quest giver | Professor Rohesia |
| Steps | 8 |
| XP | 5 |
| Rewards | Leafkin Book, Elma's Gift×3 |

## Walkthrough

| Step | Objective |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | My final exam requires me to check in with a Leafkin living just south of the Etchery. I'm to deliver him a Root Strengthening Tincture. I need to do some research first - there's a study session in the library that'll help. |
| 2 | I've got a book about Leafkin to complete my research. |
| 3 | I found Dendron and delivered the Root Strengthener Tonic. He and the other Leafkin have crafted a gift for their friend Elma. I need to collect another piece of the gift from a Leafkin in the Ochre Woods. Dendron says I won't need to offer any more tinctures to the other Leafkin. |
| 4 | I got a contribution to Elma's Root Day wreath from Chrisanthi. She asked me to see Bristlecone next. |
| 5 | I've collected all the contributions. It's time to deliver Elma's Root Day wreath, deep in the Nocturne Forest. |
| 6 | Elma loved her Root Day wreath. That fulfills my tasks for this final! I hope I did well! |
| 7 | Collected all contributions. Time to deliver Elma's Root Day wreath deep in the Nocturne Forest. |
| 8 | Elma loved her Root Day wreath. Final exam tasks complete! |
