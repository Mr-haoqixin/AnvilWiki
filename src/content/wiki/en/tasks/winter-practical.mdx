## Quest Overview

**Winter Practical** is one of the 44 quests in Songs of Glimmerwick.

| Property | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Quest giver | Professor Rohesia |
| Steps | 8 |
| XP | 5 |
| Rewards | Leafkin Book, Elma's Gift×3 |

## Walkthrough

### Step 1

My final exam requires me to check in with a Leafkin living just south of the Etchery. I'm to deliver him a Root Strengthening Tincture. I need to do some research first - there's a study session in the library that'll help.

### Step 2

I've got a book about Leafkin to complete my research.

### Step 3

I found Dendron and delivered the Root Strengthener Tonic. He and the other Leafkin have crafted a gift for their friend Elma. I need to collect another piece of the gift from a Leafkin in the Ochre Woods. Dendron says I won't need to offer any more tinctures to the other Leafkin.

### Step 4

I got a contribution to Elma's Root Day wreath from Chrisanthi. She asked me to see Bristlecone next.

### Step 5

I've collected all the contributions. It's time to deliver Elma's Root Day wreath, deep in the Nocturne Forest.

### Step 6

Elma loved her Root Day wreath. That fulfills my tasks for this final! I hope I did well!
