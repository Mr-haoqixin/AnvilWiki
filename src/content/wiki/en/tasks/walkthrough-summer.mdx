## Summer Goals Overview

Summer is the **peak growing season**, leveraging long daylight and rain to accelerate output. Core goals are **advancing friendship lines, unlocking sea travel, completing Fern quest lines**. Completing summer unlocks the **Summer** achievement.

## Phase 1: Sequential Friendship Lines

The guide recommends **advancing one friendship line at a time**. Summer recommended order:

### Clover → Mel → Florian → Nessa

1. **Clover**: Continue the tulip quest from spring, complete Business Buds
2. **Mel**: Mel's friendship line quests
3. **Florian**: Florian's book quest line (launch week 5%)
4. **Nessa**: Nessa's curse quest line (launch week 3%)

Sequential advancement avoids quest log confusion; some lines have prerequisites.

## Phase 2: Ferry Girl & Buying a Boat

Summer unlocks sea travel:

1. Complete the **Ferry Girl** quest line
2. Purchase a boat to unlock sea exploration
3. Reach new areas and islands after setting sail

## Phase 3: Fern Quest Lines

Summer advances Fern's two quest lines:

### Fern's Tea: Ginger Calming Tea

1. Ask Chrys about Ginger Calming Tea and agree to trade
2. Collect Fiddlehead, Morel, and Salt Pile
3. Brew Instagrow potion, return to Chrys to complete tea trade
4. Return to Fern to complete the tea errand

### Fern's Confrontation

1. The silent printing press produces Cacophony fragments
2. Give to Fern for 100 Gold Petals, or to Headmistress for 300 then talk to Fern
3. Meet Fern at Northern Grounds entrance, follow her to Green Sprites Headquarters

## Phase 4: Planting & Economy

Summer has the highest planting returns:

- Spread mid-tier high-value varieties
- Leverage long daylight to accelerate crop growth
- Sell items at the garden well

## Summer Missable Events Reminder

- NPC friendship quests may have time windows; advance promptly
- Summer-exclusive events miss it and wait a year
