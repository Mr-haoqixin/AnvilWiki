## Head to Bellian Moor

The original page clue says the route to **Bellian Moor** is tied to the **Summer Herbotany** camp activity. Prepare a **Gilded Toadstool** obtained through **Florian** fishing quest, then look for the fox.

## Taming Actions and Evidence Scope

The player-reported action is to right-click the **Gilded Toadstool**, then left-click the fox. This is a player report, not a confirmed complete habitat condition, and does not prove the fox can always protect crops; if the interaction does not work, first verify activity progress and item correctness.
