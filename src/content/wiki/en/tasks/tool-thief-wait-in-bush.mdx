## Choose the Bush Interaction

Go to the bush in the upper-left of the quest area and interact, then choose **wait in bush**. Players who only stood behind the bush without triggering the quest later advanced by selecting this interaction option.

## Time Hint Unconfirmed

A reply suggested trying after 9pm, but the original page did not confirm this condition as a necessary step. Prioritize confirming the bush interaction is selected, rather than only adjusting positioning.
