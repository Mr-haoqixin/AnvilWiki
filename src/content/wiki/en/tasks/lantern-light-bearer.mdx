## Craft and Equip the Hand-Held Lantern

1. At Practical Practice in Wisk, check **Nalin** lantern blueprint. The crafting guide lists the price as 400 Gold Petals, requiring Carpentry level 2.
2. Bring 10 Moon Poppy and 2 Iron bars to the crafting table. Moon Poppy is grown from Kavita Spring or Summer seeds; **Hyla** sells Iron bars at Newt's Eye.
3. Craft the Lantern, check your backpack for the finished item, then equip it in dark areas. Merely buying the blueprint does not produce a light source.

## Use Light Bearer Without a Lantern

Open the Skill Trinket Abilities tab. The ability guide lists a four-point route: Potionwork Proficiency, Spirit of Flour, Song of the Sea, then Light Bearer. Before spending points, check the connected hex and its live description. After unlocking, click the Light Bearer hex to toggle the halo on and off.

## Unlocked but Still Dark?

Before toggling, check whether Light Bearer is already active. One Steam assistant needed manual enable, another said it lit immediately after unlock; the replies did not establish a unified default for every build. Compare the visible halo at the same position. For the crafting route, check that you equipped the Lantern, not its blueprint or a Garden Lantern.
