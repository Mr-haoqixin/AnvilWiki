## Learn and Practice the Recipe

Watch for the course letter and calendar entry **Potions 101: Practical Mixes for Practical Witches**, head to the Potions Lab in the academy east wing. After the first class, select **Potion of Hardiness**, then use **Practice** to raise recipe quality. The original page records the ingredient combo as **1 twig + 2 Cut Grass**.

## Brew the Potion and Return to the Bush

Practice is for practice and does not yield a deliverable potion. After leaving the practice interface, choose **Brew** to make the potion with the same recipe, then bring it to the guard bush south of Wisk. In dev footage the bush is named Martha and accepted Exceptional Quality potion; the quality threshold may vary by version, do not treat this observation as a universal value.
