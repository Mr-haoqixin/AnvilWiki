## Trigger the Clue and Ask Characters

First pick up the **Unauthorised Flyer**, then ask **Nessa** or **Hyla** about the **Bonesand Cherry**. The original walkthrough links this dialogue to the Briny Bathhouse collection quest.

## Quantity Hint

The original walkthrough records that this cauldron step requires two cherries. After obtaining the item, return to the quest location and complete the subsequent steps per the current quest prompt.
