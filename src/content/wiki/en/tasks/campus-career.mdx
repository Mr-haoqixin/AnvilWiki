## Quest Overview

**Campus Career** is one of the 44 quests in Songs of Glimmerwick.

| Property | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Quest giver | Dean Clary |
| Steps | 3 |
| XP | 3 |
| Rewards | Basil Seeds×5, Chant of the Woodcutters |

## Walkthrough

### Step 1

Dean Clary sent a letter asking to see me. Her office is in the upper level of the Dorm Hall.

### Step 2

I'm going to be a gardener! I'm to meet Dean Clary at the garden just off the road to town.

### Step 3

I am now the Official Gardener of the Etchery Botanical Garden!
