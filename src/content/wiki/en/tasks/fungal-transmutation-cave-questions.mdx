## Get the Recipe and Buy Materials

Ask the green sprite at the Cave of Questions entrance for the recipe. A Reddit assistant described this character outside the cave; the guide calls it the cave sprite. **Hyla** sells Bone Bundle, Beetle, and Hot Essence at the Newt's Eye shop in Wisk. The guide lists prices as 80, 10, and 200 Gold Petals respectively; compare with the live shop card before buying.

## Brew the Potion Before Returning to the Cave

1. Collect Witchfire (listed as Summer or Fall forage) plus the three purchased materials.
2. At the cauldron, select Potion of Fungal Transmutation. Practice until Brew is available, then brew the actual potion.
3. Check your backpack for the potion, return to the Cave of Questions and drink it inside, then interact per the current item prompt.
4. Observe the mushroom transformation before trying to pass through.

## Red Vine Obstacle Needs a Different Solution

The current Reddit discussion conflates the fungal cave obstacle with the large red vine on the mountain path. One reply notes the vine needs Ungrow. Do not buy Fungal Transmutation materials to solve every obstacle, and do not substitute practice score for the potion. This page does not establish exact Quiet Witches locations or every cave question.
