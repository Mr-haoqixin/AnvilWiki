## Unlock the Recipe via Fairy Ring Topic

1. Reach Ochre Woods via [Ferry Girl](/tasks/ochre-woods-ferry-girl/) and talk to trapped **Codrin**.
2. Ask the librarian, Chicory, Chrys, or Nora about Fairy Ring to get Eseld's Folly.
3. Read the book, then check Potion of Befuddlement at the cauldron.

## Brew a Potion, Not Just a Practice Score

Collect 2 Wild Thyme and 2 Marjoram; the guide lists Marjoram as a Kavita seed crop. Practice the specified recipe at the cauldron until Brew is available, then Brew and check the potion in your backpack. Practice rounds do not produce offerings.

## Prepare Other Rescue Materials

Bring the potion, five Chamomile flowers, and one Iron bar back to the ring. If missing materials, use the [off-season Chamomile seeds clue](/tasks/fairy-ring-chamomile-seeds/) or the [potion shop Iron bar clue](/tasks/iron-bar-potion-shop/). Interact per the ring current offering action. This recipe answer does not establish every placement input or exact Quiet Witches locations; after rescue, the [mountain path guide](/tasks/reach-mountains-raise-earth/) covers the reported spell reward.
