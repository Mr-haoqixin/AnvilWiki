## Check Nearby Scenery First

Slowly look around the pond and garden well; the can may be occluded by scenery. Eastshade suggests trying **Space** to interact nearby; a later demo player confirmed their can was hidden behind the well and retrieved via interaction.

## Still Cannot Find It

First verify you are operating the correct save and Windows user. This clue is one confirmed hidden-location case, not a restore instruction applicable to all lost items.
