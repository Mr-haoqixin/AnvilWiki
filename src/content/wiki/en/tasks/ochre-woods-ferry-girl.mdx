## Start Ferry Girl

Head to the river and read the **Gone Fishing** sign to trigger the **Ferry Girl** quest. Then talk to the person by the Wisk pool and prepare **Blueberry Bread**.

## When the Quest Does Not Appear

The original page includes a quest-not-triggered branch, suggesting first return to the sign to confirm it has been read, then check the journal and dialogue options. The route to Ochre Woods and later obtaining a boat are different stages.
