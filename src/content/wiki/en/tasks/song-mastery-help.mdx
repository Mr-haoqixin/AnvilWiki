## Effect of Song Mastery Help

Per the developer statement quoted on the original page, **Song Mastery Help** adds score at the end of the whole song and does not change note speed. Light-blue non-perfect hits and small red error notes should also be distinguished.

## Do Not Treat Score as a Cast Threshold

Music minigame proficiency and whether a song can be used for quick casting or grant proficiency bonuses are different issues. The source did not publish a unified bonus value, nor guarantee a certain score meets all quests.
