## Story Events Do Not Auto-Expire from Clock Advance

The developer says story events do not become invalid just because the game clock advances; demo event prompts turn into **event pucks** and wait for the player to handle them.

## Note NPC Daily Schedules

Events waiting for the player does not mean all NPCs and shops are open around the clock. If a character cannot be found at night, try again during the day; do not misjudge a temporarily unavailable character as a permanently missed quest.
