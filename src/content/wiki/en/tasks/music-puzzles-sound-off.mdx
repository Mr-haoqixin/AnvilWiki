## Still Playable with Sound Off

Eastshade answer says **Songs of Glimmerwick** does not require the player to identify different pitches, so you can play with sound off. Key timing during music input and hearing pitches are not the same requirement.

## Scope of Application

This statement does not mean all future music input auto-completes, nor that every accessibility config is identical. When struggling with a specific song input, still follow in-game prompts and current version settings.
