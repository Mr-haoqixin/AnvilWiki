## Five Known Entrances

- Next to your garden shed.
- Next to **Bristlecone** house, where the flute maker lives.
- East shore of the Cypress Foothills frog pond.
- East side of the Ochre Woods lake; the reply says you need to take a boat across the lake.
- By the river between Ochre Woods and Rosewheat Fields. Starting from the position of **Kaloula** and the children in Ochre Woods, walk upstream along the river.

## When You Still Cannot Enter the Mole Hole

The potion guide says to drink Potion of the Mole next to the molehill. Its recipe comes from Secret Lives of Moles, sold by **Hyla** from Summer 2 onward, requiring 2 Snap Tater, 1 Amber Dragon, and 1 Ghost Shroom. Brew a bottle first before trying the entrance. See the separate [boat route](/tasks/get-a-boat/) for crossing the lake.

## Checked All Five but Friend Not Found?

Keep the Apology Worms and compare the delivery target in your journal with each character you find underground. The reporting player is in Winter; the assistant suggests rechecking each hole and reporting a possible bug if the friend is absent. No reliable reset, fixed schedule, or auto-complete workaround has been published. Record the entrances you checked and the game version, rather than assuming another unknown hole is needed.
