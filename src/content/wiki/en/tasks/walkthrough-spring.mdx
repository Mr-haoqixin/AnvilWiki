## Spring Goals Overview

Spring is the first-year opening season. The core goal is to **complete enrollment, unlock basic magic, and establish planting areas**. This season lays the foundation for "learning songs" and "opening land" for later high yields. Completing spring unlocks the **Spring** achievement (launch week 14%).

## Phase 1: Prologue & Enrollment

### From Saltwind Coast to Etchery

Complete prologue steps:

1. Create character, arrive at Saltwind Coast, find Abigailia to get enrollment letter
2. Head south to find Bristlecone the flute maker, collect school supplies first
3. Enter Wisk town to purchase five items: Flute, Robe, Cauldron, Potion Starter Set, Skill Trinket
4. Attend enrollment ceremony, unlock **Enrollment** achievement (launch week 84%)

### Dean Clary & Botanical Garden

After enrollment, follow Dean Clary to complete first planting:

1. Learn digging spell, plant Basil Seeds, water with watering can
2. Complete to obtain tree-cutting spell

### Roommate & Music History Class

Meet roommate Clover on Tuesday. Professor Linnea's music history class provides Pickaxe spell.

## Phase 2: Flora Festival Flower Contest

The core spring festival is the **Flora Festival Flower Contest**. Winning unlocks an achievement (launch week 13%). Preparation:

- Plant flower varieties ahead of time
- Pay attention to judging criteria (variety, quantity, arrangement)
- The festival is spring-exclusive; miss it and wait a year

See [Seasonal Guide](/info/seasonal-guide/).

## Phase 3: Clover Tulip Quest Line

Spring recommends prioritizing Clover's friendship line:

1. Talk to Clover to trigger the tulip quest
2. Gather enough tulips to deliver, unlocking **Business Buds** achievement (launch week 25%)
3. Tulip and wool quests only trigger from dialogue; talk to everyone in the first week

The guide recommends **advancing one friendship line at a time**. Clover is a recommended priority.

## Phase 4: Song of the Land Practice

Spring classes cover basic song learning. Song of the Land is a core spell:

- Learn notes and melody combinations through classes
- Different songs assist fishing, gardening, and exploration
- See [Song Mastery Help](/tasks/song-mastery-help/)

## Spring Missable Events Reminder

Developers state story events don't expire from clock advancement, but NPCs and shops have schedules:

- Flora Festival is season-exclusive; miss it and wait a year
- NPCs may not interact at night; try again during the day
- See [Missable Events & Time](/tasks/missable-events-time/)

## Spring Completion Checklist

- [x] Enroll at Etchery, unlock Enrollment
- [x] Complete first planting, obtain tree-cutting spell
- [x] Compete in Flora Festival flower contest
- [x] Advance Clover tulip quest line (Business Buds)
- [x] Learn basic songs and Song of the Land
- [x] Talk to all townsfolk to trigger available quests
