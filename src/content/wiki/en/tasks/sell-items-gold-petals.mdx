## Sell Action

Go to the well in the garden, first left-click the item to sell in the hotbar, then left-click the well. The original page notes right-click is for use or equip, not the sell flow here.

## Price and Version

The original page did not confirm a fixed sale price or unified credit timing. The sellable state of **Munchitis Treatment** is related to the **Version 1.01** update, and should not be extrapolated to mean all items can be sold.
