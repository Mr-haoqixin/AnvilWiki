## Puzzle Steps

1. Confirm the journal still shows **Ghost Treasure**, and you have found the stone with the matching symbol in Rosewheat Fields.
2. Find the alchemist near the garden entrance to get the recipe **Alchemical Resonance**.
3. Pin the recipe, then stand in front of the symbol stone and play.

## Clue Boundary

This clue comes from player description and a follow-up solution, not independent testing. If the alchemy option has not appeared, the original discussion did not confirm its unlock condition; do not treat waiting for sunset, going to another beach, or looking for silver items as verified steps.
