## Winter Goals Overview

Winter is for **social and indoor activities**. Core goals are **advancing NPC relationships, attending winter festivals, completing skill tree**. Completing all four seasons unlocks the **Trained Witch** game completion achievement. Completing winter unlocks the **Winter** achievement.

## Phase 1: Social & NPC Relationships

Winter is ideal for advancing NPC relationships:

1. Use "can't plant outside" time for making friends and indoor quests
2. Advance incomplete friendship lines, see [Romance & Relationships](/tasks/romance-relationships/)
3. Arrange wreath for Elma
4. Talk to all townsfolk to trigger winter-exclusive dialogue

## Phase 2: Winter Festivals

Attend winter festivals for exclusive rewards:

- Pay attention to festival time windows and participation conditions
- Miss winter festivals and wait a year
- See [Seasonal Guide](/info/seasonal-guide/)

## Phase 3: Complete Skill Tree

Winter is good for deepening skills:

1. Learn remaining songs, see [Song Mastery Help](/tasks/song-mastery-help/)
2. Deepen potion crafting
3. Advance remaining Ancient Shrines portions

## Phase 4: Indoor Quests & Story Deepening

Winter has dense indoor quests:

1. Advance Briny Bathhouse indoor quests
2. Handle remaining Fern quest line portions
3. Deepen story, understand game background lore

## Phase 5: Four Seasons Completion & Game Clear

Completing four seasons unlocks completion achievements:

- **Trained Witch**: Complete all four seasons
- **Witch of Glimmerwick**: All quests, endgame achievement, second-year project
- **Arch Witch**: All abilities, multi-year planning
- **Seasons of Plenty**: Cumulative 100k Gold Petals, long-term selling project
- **Musical Witch**: All song mastery, completionist project
- **Brewmaster**: All legendary brews, legendary brew pursuit

See [Achievements List & Unlock Conditions](/achievements/achievements-overview/).

## Winter Missable Events Reminder

- Winter festivals are season-exclusive; miss and wait a year
- NPC winter dialogue may have exclusive content
- See [Missable Events & Time](/tasks/missable-events-time/)

## Post-Completion: Endgame Achievement Planning

After game clear (Trained Witch unlocked), continue pursuing endgame achievements:

| Achievement | Requirement | Timeline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Witch of Glimmerwick | Complete all quests | Second year |
| Arch Witch | Unlock all abilities | Multi-year |
| Seasons of Plenty | Cumulative 100k Gold Petals | Long-term selling |
| Musical Witch | All song mastery | Completionist project |
| Brewmaster | All legendary brews | Legendary brew pursuit |

## Winter Completion Checklist

- [x] Advance NPC relationship lines
- [x] Attend winter festivals
- [x] Complete skill tree
- [x] Complete indoor quests and story deepening
- [x] All four seasons complete, unlock Trained Witch
