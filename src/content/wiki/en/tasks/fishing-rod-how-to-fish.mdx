## Get a Fishing Rod

Follow the south route out of Wisk, go past the guard bush and find the rod merchant. After buying, equip the rod, then aim at visible fish or water bubbles to cast.

## Bite and Operation Reminders

Respond promptly when the bite prompt appears, then complete the timing action per the fishing minigame. The original page mentions controller aim circle issues, but only records a player-proposed workaround, unconfirmed as an official fix or applicable to all controllers.
