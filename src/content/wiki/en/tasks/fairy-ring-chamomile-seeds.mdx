## Find Seeds in Summer

When progressing the **Fairy Ring** quest in Summer, check the seed merchant behind the town waterfall for **Chamomile** seeds. Player feedback says seeds bought outside Spring cost more.

## Stock May Vary

This info comes from a single player report, unconfirmed whether all seasons have stock or fixed prices. If currently out of stock, check the shop again later.
