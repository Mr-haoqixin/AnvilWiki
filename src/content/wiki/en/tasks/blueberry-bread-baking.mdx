## Mix Blueberry Dough

Prepare flour, blueberries, and a watering can. Use water from the watering can to knead with flour, then add blueberries to the dough to get the unbaked **Blueberry Bread** dough.

## Find a Usable Fireplace

Head to the common room near **Blue Wing** and bake at the fireplace. The original page reminds that not every fire source works as a cooking spot. There is also a Ferry Girl quest trigger clue, which is a quest progress issue and should not be treated as part of the Blueberry Bread recipe.
