## Split Action

In the public demo backpack, select an item stack, press **Shift** to split; press **Ctrl** to take out one item. Selling items requires going to the garden well, a different action from splitting stacks.

## Version Limits

This action comes from a public demo player report. The original page did not give controller equivalent keys, nor guarantee full-game key settings are identical.
