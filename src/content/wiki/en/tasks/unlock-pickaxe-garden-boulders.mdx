## Complete the Music Class First

Advance the prologue and attend **Professor Linnea** first music class. The original launch-day walkthrough places the **Pickaxe Spell** reward after the class.

## Return to the Garden to Clear Rocks

After obtaining the song, return to the garden and use the Pickaxe Spell on the boulders. If the quest still does not advance, check whether the class is complete and the song is learned and pinned.
