## Collect Cut Grass

Find the seed merchant in Wisk and buy a scythe. Per the original page, right-click to equip the scythe, then click grass tufts to harvest **Cut Grass**.

## Get Twigs

Twigs are not scythe forageables. Cast **Chant of the Woodcutters** on dead trees in the farm, then follow the woodcutter chant prompt.
