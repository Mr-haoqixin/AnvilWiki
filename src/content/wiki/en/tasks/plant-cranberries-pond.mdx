## Sow by the Pond

First clear the pond edge with a scythe, select **cranberry seeds**, and move the cursor to the circular target that appears by the water. The original page player report says you can press **Space** to sow.

## Harvest and Notes

The page player report uses a scythe to harvest Cranberries. The number of pond planting spots is a player observation, not a fixed upper limit; if you cannot sow, reconfirm the target is by the water, not on already-tilled normal land.
