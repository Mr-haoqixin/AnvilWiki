## Complete Plot Placement First

After casting the tilling spell, move the plot preview onto grass. The player-recorded controller flow is **A** to queue, **X** to confirm; do not recast before the preview appears, to avoid mistaking the spell as failed.

## Stick Directions Conflict

Different Eastshade replies mention left stick and right stick respectively; the original discussion did not explain the difference. So do not treat either direction as a confirmed fix; first check the preview and in-game prompts on your current device.
