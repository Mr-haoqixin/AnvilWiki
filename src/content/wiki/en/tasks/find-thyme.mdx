## Route Guide

Head to **Wisk** upper town, find the mud pie boy and the small path nearby blocked by trees. Use the woodcutter chant to clear the blocking trees, then follow the path into the older woman garden and look for **Thyme** there.

## Clue Boundary

This is a player-recorded foraging spot, not representative of all Thyme sources, nor confirmed for daily refresh time or season limits. If the target is not in the garden, first verify the current quest and season.
