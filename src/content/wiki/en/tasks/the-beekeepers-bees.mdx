## Walkthrough

The Beekeeper broke the gondola by causing plants to grow all over it! She's scared of heights and her bees are stuck in the mountains. She needs my help to get them back.

### Step 2

I've repaired the gondola. Now I just need something to catch the bees with.

### Step 4

I returned the Beekeeper's bees!
