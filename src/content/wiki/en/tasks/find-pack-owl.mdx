## Find the Pack Owl

Head to **Nocturne Forest**, go past the **Cave of Questions** along the path and continue to find the **Pack Owl**. The original player first searched on the mountain but got no response; this does not prove all mountain owls cannot be tamed.

## Try Taming

Prepare the **Gilded Toadstool**, right-click to use it per the player report, then left-click the Pack Owl. Habitat conditions and full taming requirements have not been confirmed.
