## Open Elma Gift Slots

Approach **Elma** and click her to open three gift slots. Place the wreath parts required by the quest into the corresponding slots to complete the gift arrangement.

## Common Omission

Simply searching the map for the wreath or trying to talk will not necessarily trigger the delivery. Player feedback indicates opening the character gift slots is the missing step.
