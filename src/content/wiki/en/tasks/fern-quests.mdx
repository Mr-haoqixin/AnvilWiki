## Fern Tea: Recipe, Trade, and Delivery

1. Ask **Chrys** about Ginger Calming Tea and agree to the trade. The potion guide says she teaches Instagrow for this quest.
2. Collect 1 Fiddlehead, 2 Morel, and 1 Salt Pile. **Hyla** sells Salt Pile at Newt's Eye; the guide lists Fiddlehead and Morel as year-round forage.
3. Practice Instagrow at the cauldron until Brew is available, then brew a bottle. Return to Chrys to complete the agreed tea trade.
4. Check your backpack for Ginger Calming Tea, then return to **Fern** to complete the tea errand. Debluing Tea treats blueface, not the drink needed for this quest.

## Fern Confrontation After Strange Pamphlets

Spoiler warning: the village guide says silent printing machines produce Cacophony fragments. Give them to Fern for 100 Gold Petals, or give them to Headmistress for 300 then talk to Fern. Both routes can start Fern confrontation. Meet Fern at the Northern Grounds gate and follow her to Green Sprites Headquarters. These are sourced route choices, not claims that rewards can be repeatedly collected.

## Lost Key Does Not Trigger After Finishing Fern Story?

A new player reported this issue — despite completing Fern dialogue and quest, Lost Key still did not trigger. No successful activation fix was obtained in this discussion. Completing the tea or confrontation does not prove every Lost Key prerequisite is met. Keep the exact journal text and NPC dialogue for follow-up; this page does not fabricate resets, key locations, or extra gift requirements.
