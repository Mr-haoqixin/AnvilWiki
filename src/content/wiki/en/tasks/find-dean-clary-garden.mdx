## Meet Dean Clary

After enrolling and sleeping one night, check upstairs in the dorm for **Dean Clary** and the letter she left. The original page reminds you to follow the current quest tracker, not treat the order in old script notes as current steps.

## Head to Botanical Garden

From the dorm, take the right road to **Botanical Garden** and complete the first planting per the instructions. When planting **basil**, use a small already-tilled plot, not a large tree plot.
