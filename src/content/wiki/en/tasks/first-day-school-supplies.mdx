## Prologue Pickup Order

![Talk to Abigailia to receive the quest](https://a.imgckr.com/2026/10/09/Xffpl.jpg)

Start from [Abigailia](/characters/abigailia/) letter, talk to [Bristlecone](/characters/bristlecone/), then head to [Wisk](/locations/wisk/).

![Items needed for the quest](https://a.imgckr.com/2026/10/10/X69OG.png)
The original launch-day flow records enrollment supplies including [cauldron](/items/cauldron/), potion starter set, [school robe](/items/school-robe/), skill trinket, and the [flute](/items/flute/) obtained afterward.

## Complete Enrollment

Follow the prologue prompts to [Newt's Eye Potion Supply](/locations/newts-eye-potion-supply/), [Swish & Stitch Clothier](/locations/swish-and-stitch-clothier/), **Practical Practice**, and **Etchery**. After shopping, return to campus, find [Heather](/characters/heather/) and head to the dorm to rest.
