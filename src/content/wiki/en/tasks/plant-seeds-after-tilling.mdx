## Choose the Right Plot

The tilling song only prepares the ground. Select seeds and manually place them into small plots; the original page uses **basil** as an example, reminding not to put normal seeds into large tree plots.

## Fill Water from the Pond

Take the watering can to the small pond to fill water, then water the sown land. The garden well and water source are not the same purpose.
