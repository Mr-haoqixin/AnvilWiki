## Part 1: The Silence of the Universe | Etchery | Town

### Worldview Background

The universe once liked to hear all beings sing, but when living things sang of tragedy and pain, the universe silenced all things (the Silence). This setting is the starting point of the entire game story.

### Arrive at Saltwind Coast

After creating a character, arrive by boat at [Saltwind Coast](/locations/saltwind-coast/). Walk left to find the talking Dragon Statue, which welcomes you to Saltwind Coast. Continue left to find [Abigailia](/characters/abigailia/), the headmistress of [Etchery University](/locations/etchery-university/).

### Get the Flute

Abigailia gives a letter; head south to find the flute maker [Bristlecone](/characters/bristlecone/). Bristlecone asks you to collect school supplies first before coming back for the flute.

### Enter Wisk Town

Enter [Wisk](/locations/wisk/) town and talk to the woman at the gate to get a map.

## Part 2: The Wisk | Getting Into Etchery | First Job | Common Room Meeting

### Items Needed for Enrollment

Enrollment items include: Flute, Robe, Cauldron, Potion Starter Set, Skill Trinket. Purchase locations are Newt's Eye Potion Supply, Swish & Stitch Clothier, and Practical Practice. See [First Day of School: Pick Up Supplies and Find Dorm](/tasks/first-day-school-supplies/).

### Enrollment Ceremony

After finding the students, follow them to the university grounds for the enrollment ceremony. [Heather](/characters/heather/) is the dorm supervisor.

### Dean Clary and the Botanical Garden

[Dean Clary](/characters/dean-clary/) assigns botanical garden work. Learn the tilling spell, plant Basil Seeds, and water with the watering can. After completion, obtain the tree-cutting spell. See [Find Dean Clary and Botanical Garden](/tasks/find-dean-clary-garden/).

### Sell Items

Dropping things into the well sells them; see [Sell Items at the Gold Petals Well](/tasks/sell-items-gold-petals/).

### Roommate and Music History Class

On Tuesday, meet roommate Clover. Professor Linnea music history class grants the Pickaxe spell; see [Professor Linnea: Music History Professor and Pickaxe Spell](/characters/linnea/).
