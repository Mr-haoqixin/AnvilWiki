## Walkthrough

| Step | Objective |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Mel sent a message in a bottle asking me to come by for a visit at the Shell. |
| 2 | Mel's been stung by a jellyfish. She asked me to brew a Potion of Healing for her. |
| 3 | The Potion of Healing is ready! I should take it to Mel. |
| 4 | I healed Mel's foot, but she is still salty about living above water. There's not much more I can do right now. |
| 5 | I got word from Mel that she is trapped on a shark-ridden island south of Glimmerwick and needs help! It doesn't sound safe to swim there. |

| 7 | Mel and Lusina left to live in a merfolk town. |
