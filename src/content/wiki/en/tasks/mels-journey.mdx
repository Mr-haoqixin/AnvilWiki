## Quest Overview

**Mel's Journey** is one of the 44 quests in Songs of Glimmerwick.

| Property | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Quest giver | Mel |
| Steps | 7 |
| XP | 5 |
| Rewards | Potion of Healing recipe, Amulet of Boat Summoning |

## Walkthrough

### Step 1

Mel sent a message in a bottle asking me to come by for a visit at the Shell.

### Step 2

Mel's been stung by a jellyfish. She asked me to brew a Potion of Healing for her.

### Step 3

The Potion of Healing is ready! I should take it to Mel.

### Step 4

I healed Mel's foot, but she is still salty about living above water. There's not much more I can do right now.

### Step 5

I got word from Mel that she is trapped on a shark-ridden island south of Glimmerwick and needs help! It doesn't sound safe to swim there.

### Step 6

Mel's stuck on an island and needs me to brew some Shark Repellant!

### Step 7

Mel and Lusina left to live in a merfolk town.
