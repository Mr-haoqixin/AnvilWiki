## Get the Blueprint and Build the Mill

1. Go to Practical Practice in Wisk and find **Nalin**. The guide lists the Wheatmill blueprint at 450 Gold Petals, requiring Carpentry level 2.
2. At the crafting table, use 10 Log, 5 Feather, and 10 Stone to craft the Wheatmill.
3. Plant Wild Wheat with Kavita Wheat Seeds; the cooking guide lists these seeds as year-round available.
4. Place the mill, interact and choose Open to load Wild Wheat. The guide lists each Wild Wheat yielding one Flour; check output before use.

## Use Flour to Complete the Ferry Bread Request

Mix Flour with water and blueberries, then bake the dough per the [blueberry bread guide](/tasks/blueberry-bread-baking/). The current discussion disagrees on whether the baker ever gave free flour. Treat it as a possible dialogue reward, not a guaranteed substitute for producing flour yourself. If the request itself is missing, first check the [Gone Fishing sign](/tasks/ochre-woods-ferry-girl/).

## Bought the Blueprint but No Flour?

Buying the blueprint does not mean you crafted the mill. Check Carpentry level, completed Wheatmill item, placement, and loaded Wild Wheat. Wait for actual output rather than looking for Flour in an empty machine. The Adept Wheatmill reward after The Cursed Mill is a later machine, not a prerequisite for this alternative.
