## Where to Buy the Tea

After the blue mushroom appearance appears, go to Wisk and find **Chrys**, choose the dialogue about the **Sorrowspore** state. The price recorded on the original page is **50 Gold Petals**. Professor Sylvine points the player to Chrys rather than providing an alternative potion recipe.

## Drink It After Buying

After obtaining **Debluing Tea**, use it from your backpack or hotbar; simply buying it does not automatically remove the appearance. The original page distinguishes this state from Fern ginger tea related quest.
