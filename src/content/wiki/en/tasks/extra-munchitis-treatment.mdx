## Version 1.01 Item Adjustment

**Version 1.01** makes **Munchitis Treatment** droppable and sellable. After updating, you can replay an earlier date as needed, then dispose of the extra copy from your backpack.

## Do Not Confuse with Old Chest Claim

Players mentioned storing the potion in a dorm chest, but that is another unconfirmed report not validated by this patch. The explicit change in Version 1.01 is droppable and sellable, and should not be extended to mean all items can be stored in chests.
