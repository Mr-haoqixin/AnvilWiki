## Learn the Shears Recipe First

Head to the **Summer** camp activity and learn the **Shears** recipe from a professor per player replies. Simply clicking the sheep in Rosewheat Field or using a **Scythe** will not substitute for this tool.

## Distinguish the Two Tools

Scythe is for harvesting plants; shearing sheep requires Shears. If the recipe is not yet unlocked, first complete the corresponding camp activity progress.
