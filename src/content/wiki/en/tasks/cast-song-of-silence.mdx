## Manual Play Steps

1. Open the menu and pin **Song of Silence**.
2. Stand near the mill and press **R** to enter instrument mode.
3. Play the notes displayed on screen for the pinned song.

## Key Notes

The keyboard sequence that appeared in discussion is `d, k, f, ;, l`, which only applies to the corresponding keyboard configuration. Do not treat it as a controller button table; the original page records a player report, not a unified mapping for all devices.
