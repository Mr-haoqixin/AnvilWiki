## Walkthrough

| Step | Objective |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The Temple of Silence is sealed shut. The carving by the fountain said I must drink from a chalice, but there was no chalice to be found. |
| 2 | An owl has taken the chalice for himself. He has formally agreed to trade if I can catch him a Sunray Salmon from the river. |
| 3 | I've got the Sunray Salmon - now I can trade with the owl and get back the chalice. |
| 4 | I got the chalice back from the owl. Now I can drink from the Temple of Silence fountain. |
| 5 | The temple door has unlocked! |
