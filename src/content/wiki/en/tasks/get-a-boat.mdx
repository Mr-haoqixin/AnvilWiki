## Unlock the Old Boat Purchase

After completing the **Ferry Girl** quest, the original page records you can check **Dee** old boat trade from **Summer 8**. Prepare **2,500 Gold Petals** and go by the current dialogue and shop state.

## Distinguish Three Boat Progressions

Using the river ferry, buying a private boat, and later obtaining a charm to summon a boat are different matters. Completing Ferry Girl or riding the ferry does not mean you already own a summonable boat.
