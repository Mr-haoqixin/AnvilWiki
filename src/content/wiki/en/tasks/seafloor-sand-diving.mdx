## Search Bubble Spots

Players dove at sea surface bubble spots near **Mel's Shell** and around **Newcomer Beach** and reported finding **Seafloor Sand**. Watch for surface bubbles while exploring the coast; not finding it once does not mean the target spot is wrong.

## Version 1.02 Drop Rate Adjustment

The source page records **Version 1.02** raised the Seafloor Sand appearance rate, but did not give a fixed probability or guarantee every dive yields it. After finding the route, still go by the current version actual results.
