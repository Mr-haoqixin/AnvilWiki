## Place a Teleport Point

Use rope in **Nocturne Forest** to establish a fast travel point. Player feedback says you can later move the rope to another location.

## Do Not Treat Drop as Use

Dropping rope from the backpack to the ground is different from using it to establish a fast travel point. Per the original page, place via the item use action and confirm the game shows the corresponding location marker.
