## Check Shrine Progress

After reporting to the anthropologist, if **Ancient Shrines** still shows in progress, check whether the **Seventh shrine** achievement at the temple is complete.

## Find Salmon in Fall

The player reply also noted that in **Fall** you can look for salmon in the river by the owl. This clue supplements the quest and does not mean other seasons have no salmon.
