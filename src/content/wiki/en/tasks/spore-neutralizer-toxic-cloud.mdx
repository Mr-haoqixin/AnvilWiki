## Use the Bug Catcher to Collect Materials

Equip the Bug Catcher at the forest spores and use the current catch interaction. A player who had cleared the Cave of Questions still missed this tool, then posted a self-solution: Bug Catcher. Check your backpack to confirm it contains Toxic Spores. The reply did not give controller keys or a fixed spawn rate.

## Check Class, Materials, and Potion

1. If Spore Neutralizer is missing, check the Summer 4 Advanced Potions class and known recipes.
2. Bring 3 Toxic Spores and 1 Lavender to the cauldron.
3. Practice the recipe until Brew is available, then brew the actual potion.
4. Return to the toxic cloud blocking the path. Equip the potion and interact with the cloud, or drag the potion onto the cloud, as the guide describes.

## Toxic Cloud Still Blocking?

Check the potion, selected target, and recipe name; catching spores or completing practice does not itself mean applying the potion. This answer targets the toxic cloud, not every forest obstacle or the de-spore spell the poster mentioned. If this specific cloud persists after application, keep its location and your build version for follow-up, rather than consuming unrelated potions.
