## Unlock the Stand Opposite the Magic Well

Per the community guide, complete three items at a cooking fire or Pickling Machine. Milled Flour does not count toward this unlock. Accept Amka offer when she visits the garden; the stand appears opposite the magic well after two mornings. The guide does not require three different recipes.

## Place Food and Collect Overnight Income

1. Take a completed homemade baked good or pickle, set quest food aside separately.
2. Select the item and place it on the stand, same action as the [selling well](/tasks/sell-items-gold-petals/).
3. Check overnight income. The guide reports a 20% stand bonus; the Steam replier also recalled 20% but with uncertain qualifiers.

## When the Stand Refuses Your Items

Shop-bought food is excluded. Put homemade food in a separate stack: the guide warns merging it into a bought-food stack may cause it to be counted as purchased. Flour from your own mill can be accepted after stand unlock, even though Flour itself does not count toward unlock. For first baking, see the [knead and fireplace steps](/tasks/blueberry-bread-baking/).

## When Homemade Items Still Fail

Record the exact item, stack history, and installed version. These sources explain intended usage; they do not establish a universal interaction bug fix. Do not discard quest items or reload progress to test the stand.
