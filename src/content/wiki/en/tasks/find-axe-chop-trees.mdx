## Chop Dead Trees with the Song

In the public demo, the original page does not require the player to pick up a physical axe first. Cast **Chant of the Woodcutters** on the dead trees in the farm and aim at the tree.

## If the Tree Does Not Fall Immediately

Player feedback mentions the enchanted axe may take time to reach the target. First confirm the target is indeed the dead tree, then wait for the animation to complete; do not assume you need to find a physical axe because of this.
