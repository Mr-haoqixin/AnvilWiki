## Craft the Garden Storage Chest

Find the **Practical Practice** recipe and reach **Carpenter 2**. The original page records the recipe price as **100 Gold Petals**; after learning it, craft the chest and place it in the garden.

## Backpack Expansion Is a Separate System

Player feedback mentions the **giant mole** in the garden and skill points may relate to ten extra backpack slots. This info is a player clue, not the same mechanism as the chest recipe or storage capacity; the original page also reminds that not all quest items can be stored in the chest.
