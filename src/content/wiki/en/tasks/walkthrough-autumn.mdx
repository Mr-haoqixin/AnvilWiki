## Autumn Goals Overview

Autumn is the **harvest and trading** season. Core goals are **advancing Briny Bathhouse quest line, completing Ancient Shrines, solving Inquiry Cavern, handling curse quests**. Completing autumn unlocks the **Autumn** achievement.

## Phase 1: Briny Bathhouse Quest Line

Autumn's core quest line unfolds at Briny Bathhouse:

1. Enter Briny Bathhouse and talk to relevant NPCs
2. Advance bathhouse-related quests
3. Obtain Bathhouse XP and Water Wear

## Phase 2: Ancient Shrines Progression

### Seventh Shrine

Ancient Shrines quest has key progression in autumn:

1. Report shrine progress to the anthropologist
2. If Ancient Shrines still shows in-progress, check the **Seventh shrine** achievement
3. See [Ancient Shrines Quest Completion](/tasks/ancient-shrines-quest-completion/)

### Autumn Salmon

Autumn-exclusive clue: find salmon in the river next to the owl. Salmon is a key item for the shrine quest; other seasons may not have it.

## Phase 3: Inquiry Cavern

Inquiry Cavern is an autumn puzzle quest:

1. Solve the puzzle box, take your time
2. Inquiry Cavern achievement launch week unlock rate ~3%

## Phase 4: Curse-Related Quests

Autumn advances curse-related quest lines:

1. **Nessa's Curse**: Nessa friendship line curse handling (launch week 3%)
2. **Debluing Tea**: Cures blue-face condition
3. **Silent Printing Press**: Produces Cacophony fragments, related to Fern confrontation

## Phase 5: Harvest & Trading

Autumn has the highest economic activity density:

- Convert spring and summer investments into funds and resource reserves
- Prepare for winter and next year's opening
- See [Seasonal Guide](/info/seasonal-guide/)

## Autumn Missable Events Reminder

- Autumn salmon is season-exclusive; miss it and wait a year
- Shrine quest needs autumn progression for the seventh shrine
- See [Missable Events & Time](/tasks/missable-events-time/)

## Autumn Completion Checklist

- [x] Advance Briny Bathhouse quest line
- [x] Complete Seventh Shrine achievement
- [x] Obtain autumn salmon and deliver
- [x] Complete Inquiry Cavern puzzle
- [x] Handle Nessa curse-related quests
- [x] Autumn harvest and trading to recoup funds
