## Walkthrough

### Step 1

Clover has a plan for raising money to fix her fireplace. She asked me to grow her 10 tulips in time for market day. I've added it to my calendar.

### Step 2

Clover will be selling... something in the town market.

### Step 3

Clover sold her tulip rock art for some gold. The patron also gave her a strange green gem. He said it would be handy on campus. Clover gifted it to me.
