## Quest Overview

**Nessa's Curse** is one of the 44 quests in Songs of Glimmerwick.

| Property | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Quest giver | Mary |
| Steps | 6 |
| XP | 5 |
| Rewards | Clover Potion Homework, Potion of Speed recipe |

## Walkthrough

### Step 1

I told Mary and Davin I'd find out when Nessa's tutoring hours are. She's probably in the potions lab. Mary also wants to know Nessa's hobbies.

### Step 2

I should report back to Mary and Davin about how things went with Nessa.

### Step 3

There doesn't seem to be anything more to do about this situation for the moment.

### Step 4

Clover, Mary and Davin are all sick and believe Nessa cursed them. We need to keep an eye out for suspicious activity from her.

### Step 6

Nessa told me about her twin sister and the accident that took her powers. It turns out Nessa didn't curse anyone on campus though. She gave me a recipe for Speed Potions!
