## Quest Overview

**Nessa's Curse** is one of the 44 quests in Songs of Glimmerwick.

| Property | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Quest giver | Mary |
| Steps | 6 |
| XP | 5 |
| Rewards | Clover Potion Homework, Potion of Speed recipe |

## Walkthrough

| Step | Objective |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | I told Mary and Davin I'd find out when Nessa's tutoring hours are. She's probably in the potions lab. Mary also wants to know Nessa's hobbies. |
| 2 | I should report back to Mary and Davin about how things went with Nessa. |
| 3 | There doesn't seem to be anything more to do about this situation for the moment. |
| 4 | Clover, Mary and Davin are all sick and believe Nessa cursed them. We need to keep an eye out for suspicious activity from her. |
| 5 | Talk to Clover in Dorm Kitchen. After we accidentally summoned a demon to the school, Dean Clary was very upset. Talk to Dean Clary in Dean's Office. |
| 6 | Nessa told me about her twin sister and the accident that took her powers. It turns out Nessa didn't curse anyone on campus though. She gave me a recipe for Speed Potions! |
