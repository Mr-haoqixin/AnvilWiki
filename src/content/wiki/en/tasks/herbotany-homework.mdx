## Walkthrough

| Step | Objective |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Our class is preparing a float for the Seaflower Festival. Professor Rohesia asked us to collect flowers. They can be from the wild or grown from seeds. There will be a special prize for whoever collects the most flowers! |
| 2 | After collecting enough flowers, deliver them to Professor Rohesia. |
| 3 | The float decoration is complete, quest finished. |
