## Recipe Scope and Version Notes

This page compiles the cooking and pickling recipes of **Songs of Glimmerwick**, and does not include finished goods bought directly from shops. The recipe table is based primarily on [Weki\u2019s Cooking index](https://songsofglimmerwick.weki.gg/cooking), cross-checked against [Neoseeker\u2019s cooking guide](https://www.neoseeker.com/songs-of-glimmerwick/Cooking) and [recipe page](https://www.neoseeker.com/songs-of-glimmerwick/Cooking_Recipes). Weki is an unofficial, game-data-based resource site, not an official list published by the developer; the page lists 41 recipe trees. This page was compiled on 2026-10-13; specific item names and available locations should be verified against the current game version.

Early walkthroughs and newer indexes show minor name differences, for example Blueberry Bun was once written as Blueberry Muffin, and Dandelion Cookie was once written as Dandelion Bread. The table below uses the current Weki index names; available sources are insufficient to confirm whether these differences come from version updates.

## Basic Cooking Flow

1. Obtain **Flour** from the mill merchant, or grind **Wild Wheat** into flour using a **Wheatmill**. Mill supply may be affected by the progress of the **The Cursed Mill** quest; if it is temporarily unavailable, see [crafting a Wheatmill and obtaining flour](/tasks/flour-wheatmill-cursed-mill/).
2. Combine **Flour** with a **Waterskin** or **Watering Can** filled with water to make **Dough**. Sources record that making dough consumes one unit of water.
3. Plain **Dough** placed in a **Cook Fire** produces **Bread**. To make flavored baked goods, combine any material from the table below with one unit of Dough to form the corresponding dough or batter, then place it in the Cook Fire.
4. The special **Fisherman\u2019s Pie** is first made into **Uncooked Fisherman\u2019s Pie** using any fish from the table, then placed in the Cook Fire. Cooking does not require a minigame; reference sources record roughly 50 in-game seconds per item, and **Spirit of Flour** can shorten this to about 25 seconds.

Cooking locations are fixed hearths, not campfires that can be placed anywhere. Listed locations include the Etchery Common Room, the Library, and the summer-open Wisk **Cup & Kettle**; hearth availability may depend on season or quest progress. No independent per-recipe unlock conditions were found, which is not enough to assert that no progress limits exist in the game.

### Fisherman\u2019s Pie and the Unconfirmed Plum Dough

Any of the following fish can be combined with dough to make **Uncooked Fisherman\u2019s Pie**, then baked in a Cook Fire:

**Yodeling Catfish, Sunray Salmon, Blue Trout, Bonefin, Brinebelly, Bubblehead, Emerald Crab, Golden Trill, Horn Fish, Iron Tail, Jade Butterfly Fish, Lantern Fish, Merlin\u2019s Beard, Merman\u2019s Folly, Mud Fish, Murkwater Vampire, Prismatic Squid, River Pixie, Royal Trout, Saltwater Harp, Sea Peony, Spectral Eel.**

The index also lists **Plum Dough \u2192 Plum Tart**, but the method for obtaining or crafting Plum Dough has not been confirmed. For now, do not treat directly combining any plum with Dough as a verified recipe.

## Pickling Machine Pickling Recipes

Pickling does not use a Cook Fire: place a raw material into a **Pickling Machine** to produce one corresponding finished item. The index lists one result per raw material; processing takes about 50 in-game seconds.

### Pickles

The following materials each produce **Pickles**:

**Beet, Carrot, Colossal Pumpkin, Cucumber, Eggplant, Fiddlehead, Gloomroot, Kale, Nocturne Squash, Onion, Quicksilver Cabbage, Scarlet Maize, Sleepless Peas, Snap Tater, Spring Tuber, Spundle Bean, Stinging Nettle, Tomato, Wild Leeks, Yam.**

### Pickled Fruit Medley

The following materials each produce **Pickled Fruit Medley**:

**Blueberry, Cocoa Bean, Cranberry, Dark Plum, Ember Hearts, Rhubarb, Strawberry, Whistling Peach, Wild Blackberries, Wild Blueberries, Winter Berry.**

### Pickles of the Sea

The following materials each produce **Pickles of the Sea**:

**Bane of Bard, Blue Trout, Bonefin, Grizzled Seaweed, Mussel, Plaited Kelp, Scallop, Sea Beans, Sea Toes, Seastar, Urchin, Whistlers.**

**Ginger** pickled on its own produces **Ginger Beer**.

## Machines, Effects and Common Pitfalls

- **Pickling Machine** is separate from the cooking recipes themselves: the Neoseeker guide says it can be obtained by completing quests related to the **Briny Bathhouse**. Please refer to the quest log and the current version\u2019s rewards.
- **Wheatmill** can grind Wild Wheat into Flour; crafting and unlock conditions may involve skill levels, blueprints and materials, see the [mill and flour guide](/tasks/flour-wheatmill-cursed-mill/).
- Cooked items can restore mana; **Baker\u2019s Touch** increases the mana restoration effect of baked goods. **Esteemed Baker** is recorded in walkthroughs as increasing food sale prices. These are ability effects, not required conditions for recipes.
- Pickles and Ginger Beer are machine products and should not be treated as Cook Fire baked goods; ready-made food sold in shops also does not belong to the crafting recipes listed on this page.
- If you want to sell homemade food, see [unlocking and using Amka\u2019s bake stand](/tasks/garden-bake-stand/); the stand\u2019s quest conditions and recipe unlocks are two separate things.
- The gathering locations, seasons and inventory of each raw material are not exhaustively listed in the above indexes. When materials are missing, first check the item description, season and shop inventory; do not treat unverified gathering locations as fixed rules.
