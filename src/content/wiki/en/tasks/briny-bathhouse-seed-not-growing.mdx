## Play the Song Next to the Seed

After sowing the seed near **Acorn**, pin the **Lakedew Lily Song**, approach the seed and play it manually. Player reports say this action is still needed after sowing.

## When the Song Is Not Yet Learned

If the song is not yet unlocked, first follow the birdsong route to learn the corresponding melody, then return to the seed and try again. The original page does not provide other fertilization or wait-time conditions.
