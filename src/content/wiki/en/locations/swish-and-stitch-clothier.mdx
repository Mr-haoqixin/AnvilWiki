## Use in the Enrollment Flow

The launch-day prologue walkthrough lists Swish & Stitch Clothier as the clothing shop in [Wisk](/locations/wisk/), and directs players to claim the [school robe](/items/school-robe/) here.

Existing sources do not describe the shop's other goods or the robe's stat effects, so this entry only records the confirmed prologue use.

For the full supplies route, see [First Day School Supplies walkthrough](/tasks/first-day-school-supplies/).
