## Enrollment and Fast Travel

According to the reference walkthrough, going to Etchery University to enroll is the first step. The 84% unlock rate of the Enrollment achievement indicates almost everyone finds it; do it first so the map's fast-travel anchors light up.

## Academic-Year Rhythm

The academy year splits the game into four seasons. Courses teach songs and mechanics, clubs are the social layer, and festivals are the season finale. See [Game Overview](/info/game-overview/).

For the enrollment flow, see [First Day School Supplies walkthrough](/tasks/first-day-school-supplies/). For the enrollment achievement distribution, see [Achievements Overview](/achievements/achievements-overview/).
