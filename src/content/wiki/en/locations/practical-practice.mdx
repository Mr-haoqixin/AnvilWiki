## Shop Overview

Practical Practice is the crafting-bench shop in the town of [Wisk](/locations/wisk/), run by Nalin. The Skill Trinket, one of the school supplies, is obtained here, making it a required stop on the prologue shopping route.

## Crafting Blueprints

The shop sells various crafting blueprints:

- **Lantern blueprint**: 400 Gold Petals, requires Carpentry level 2. After crafting the Lantern you can complete [Too Dark? Craft a Lantern or Unlock Light Bearer](/tasks/lantern-light-bearer/).
- **Wheatmill blueprint**: 450 Gold Petals. After crafting the Wheatmill you can resolve [No Flour After the Mill Is Cursed](/tasks/flour-wheatmill-cursed-mill/).

## Crafting Requirements

Crafting requires the relevant materials and Carpentry level. Before buying a blueprint, confirm your Carpentry level meets the requirement, so you don't buy a blueprint you can't yet craft.
