## The Town
![Map](https://a.imgckr.com/2026/10/09/Xflue.jpg)
In the prologue, the player enters Wisk by continuing right from the flute-maker [Bristlecone](/characters/bristlecone/)'s shop. The walkthrough records that a female NPC at the town entrance provides a map.

## School-Supply Shops

Multiple items on the school-supplies list are distributed across Wisk's shops: the cauldron and potion starter kit are at [Newt's Eye Potion Supply](/locations/newts-eye-potion-supply/), the school robe is at [Swish & Stitch Clothier](/locations/swish-and-stitch-clothier/), and the Skill Trinket is at Practical Practice.

For the collection order and revisit steps, see [First Day School Supplies walkthrough](/tasks/first-day-school-supplies/).
