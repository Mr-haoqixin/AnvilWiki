## Arriving at Saltwind Coast

Saltwind Coast is the island reached by boat after character creation, and the starting area of the game's prologue. After landing, walk left to begin exploring.

## Dragon Statue

Walking left leads to the talking Dragon Statue, which welcomes you to Saltwind Coast. This is the first landmark of the prologue.

## Finding Abigailia

Continue left to find [Abigailia](/characters/abigailia/), the head of [Etchery University](/locations/etchery-university/). Talk to her to formally begin the prologue flow, then head to [Wisk](/locations/wisk/) to pick up school supplies.
