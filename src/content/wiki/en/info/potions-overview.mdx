## How Brewing Works

Put the ingredients in a [cauldron](/items/cauldron/) and play the brewing practice. Your score decides the potion's quality, and better quality sells for more and lasts longer.

Five quality tiers:

1. Poor Quality
2. Average Quality
3. High Quality
4. Exceptional Quality
5. Legendary Quality

## Learn Sources

- **NPC teachers**: Nessa (Fire Art Potion 1/2, Potion of Speed), Florian (Infernal Snail Repellent), Chrys (Instagrow), Mel (Potion of Healing), Professor Sylvine (Spore Neutralizer)
- **Classes**: Potions 101 (Potion of Hardiness), Potions 102 (Insta Ungrow), Summer Advanced Potions (Spore Neutralizer), Winter study session (Root Strengthener Tonic)
- **Quests**: Fairy Ring quest (Potion of Befuddlement), conversation (Potion of Fungal Transmutation)
- **Books**: Plants of the Western Isles (Munchitis Treatment), Secret Lives of Moles (Potion of the Mole)
- **No specific source**: Fisherman's Brew, Potion of Detransformation, Potion of Growing, Shark Repellant
