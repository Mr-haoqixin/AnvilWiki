## Which fish should you catch for money?

Rank every catch by its shop value and the answer is the **Spectral Eel** (35 gold, 42 with the Fishmonger ability), with **Merman's Folly** close behind at 30. Both are 1/10 ultra-rare, so for steady income the **Sea Moth** is the practical pick: it is only a 6/10 common, bites in the ocean all through the day, and still sells for 25. Every price below comes from community game-file data, version 1.06.

## What is the single best fishing spot for gold?

**The ocean at dusk.** From 18:00 to 22:00 an ocean spot offers Lantern Fish (25), Prismatic Squid (15), Inky Puffer (10), Jelly Bell (7) and a shot at Merman's Folly (30), so its evening pool holds almost every valuable saltwater species. During the day the same waters add Sea Moth (25), Horn Fish (20) and Jade Butterfly Fish (18). For a river or lake farm, fish the evening for Spectral Eel (35) and Murkwater Vampire (15).

No season or weather matters: every species bites in all four seasons and in any weather, so you can chase the top of this table from Spring 1 to Winter 14. One quirk from the data — a fishing area named River is coded as ocean water and yields ocean fish, and pond-type fish actually appear in rivers and lakes.

## How do you make each catch worth more?

Three effects stack on top of the base prices, and one brew shifts the odds toward the expensive rows:

| Booster | Effect on income | Data |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fishmonger ability | Fish sell for 20% more | 35 → 42 on a Spectral Eel |
| Treasure Finder ability | 5% chance per catch of 20–50 gold petals instead of the fish | Per catch |
| Song of the Sea ability | A perfect 100% fishing song lands 2–3 fish at once | Per perfect play |
| Legendary Fisherman's Brew | Mana per catch drops to 3.2; rare fish surge | 140 s duration |

The brew quality tiers cut the 16-mana catch cost by 20% each, from 16 down to 3.2, and they reshape the bite table: with a Legendary brew in the ocean during the day, Merman's Folly rises from 2.6% to 17.6% of bites while Brinebelly falls from 20.5% to 3.9%. For a pure gold run, drink the best Fisherman's Brew you can brew, hook the Fishmonger ability from the [ability tree](/info/abilities-overview/), and empty the evening ocean.

## Is fishing better money than farming?

It is competitive but not the ceiling. A single Spectral Eel at 42 (with Fishmonger) beats any single harvested crop, yet the top tree crop returns 65 gold per day per tile on repeat harvests, as the [crop profit guide](/info/crop-profit-guide/) breaks down. Fishing's edge is that it costs mana, not seeds — 16 per catch, refunded on failure — and needs no farmland, so it is the best income source on rainy expeditions and festival days. The [fishing rod page](/items/fishing-rod/) explains the cast-to-catch loop.

All prices, probabilities and buff numbers above were measured from community game-file data, version 1.06.
