## Calendar Overview

Four seasons, 14 days each, 56 days total. Classes and festivals occur on fixed dates; story nights appear after letters and dialogue add them. Time freezes during calendar events, so no need to rush.

Weather is the same every playthrough: **12 rain days, 10 snow days (6 heavy), 24 fog days**. Rain or snow auto-waters crops.

## Teahouse Nights

The teahouse opens on Summer Day 1. Each subsequent show is 10 days after the previous. Doors open at 17:00, show runs 18:00–22:00.

| Show | Performer | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 4 | Rose and Raven | Winter Day 3 |

## Weekly Recurrences

- **Seed Co-op Box**: Every Sunday after joining the Seed Co-op
- **Dorm Mud Pie Box**: Every Monday after unlocking
- **Practice Workshop**: Tue, Thu, Fri, Sun (craft beehives, benches, chests, cushions, fountains, garden lanterns, stone paths, trellises, wheatmills, etc.)
- **Miller**: Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat (flour 20g, wheat seeds 20g)

## After Winter

After Winter Day 14, the year enters **endless spring**: seasons stop changing, no more snow, calendar continues counting months.
