## What can you upgrade in the garden shed?

Two things: the interior, through the remodel book, and the building itself, through Ivarice. Remodeling unlocks after the shed ghost story ends — the day after Ivarice starts work, the Shed Remodelling letter arrives and the remodel book inside the shed opens for business. The rules are strict: you pay for every category you change, the first option (the original or empty look) is always free, and switching back to a style you already bought charges you again. The category-level summary lives in the [shed overview](/info/shed-overview/); this page prices all 49 options individually, from community game-file data, version 1.06.

Buying everything in the book costs **6,290 gold**. The two big-ticket categories are the table sets (1,880) and rugs (1,320) — over half the total for pure looks.

## Which upgrades do something besides look good?

Three purchases add real function to the shed. The **Large Chest** at 150 is the cheapest storage in the game and worth buying the moment the book opens. The **Potion Station** at 350 gives you an in-shed cauldron spot, pairing with the brewing advice in the [potions overview](/info/potions-overview/). The **Crafting Table** at 550 is the priciest single option and puts carpentry on site, next to the blueprint shopping plan in the [crafting recipe sources](/info/crafting-recipe-sources/). The **Bookshelves** (100 each) round out the study corner. Everything else — walls, trim, floors, rugs, tables, plants and the seasonal decorations — is cosmetic, so style can wait for the profits from the [crop profit guide](/info/crop-profit-guide/).

## What does Ivarice sell?

Ivarice runs three sequential upgrades on the building, and only the first exists in v1.06:

| Upgrade | Price | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fireplace | 1,000 | Available — finished the next morning after payment |
| Library wall | — | Not reachable in v1.06 |
| Cauldron station | — | Not reachable in v1.06 |

Pay him 1,000 gold and the fireplace is done when you wake. The later two slots are planned in the files but cannot be reached, so budget for the fireplace and treat the rest as future patches.

## Is the shed worth 7,290 gold?

The functional core — chest, potion station, crafting table and fireplace — runs 2,050 gold and genuinely changes daily routines. The remaining 5,240 buys identity, and because repurchased styles charge again, the data rewards committing to one look instead of window-shopping every season. The ghost story that gates all of this also reveals the Flower Farmer ability, mapped in [how to unlock every ability](/info/how-to-unlock-every-ability/).
