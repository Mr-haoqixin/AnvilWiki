## Mail System

Your mailbox at the Etchery, letter by letter. **Reading a letter** is what adds its events to your calendar and its entries to your journal.

Letters dated in the future stay hidden until their morning arrives.

- **33 letters** — 27 arrive on fixed days, 6 depend on your progress

**Prof Clary's Note** (Spring 2)

>
> It's my pleasure to welcome you to the Etchery. All students are required to hold a job on campus while enrolled in courses. Please meet me first thing today to get you set up with a new position. My office is just up the stairs from the dorm hall, through the smaller door between the statues.
>
> Dean Clary

**Music Lesson Letter** (Spring 2)

> Welcome to the Etchery! All first season witches have been enrolled in the following course:
>
> Introductory Musical Craft: The Study of Music for Magical Purposes
>
> Your first day of class will take place on Tuesday in the Music Hall. Find this at the top floor of the west wing of the castle. All that is needed is your flute.

**Potion Lesson Letter** (Spring 3)

### Progress-Dependent Letters (6)

**Acorn Dragon Watch** — After saving the water dragon ([Briny Bathhouse](/tasks/briny-bathhouse/) quest), once [Heather](/characters/heather/)'s note scene is available. Dated for the next free day.

> I've heard some rumors about dragons returning to the island. It just may be the one we hatched! Meet me at Ochre Lake at 9pm - they are nocturnal so we may have a better chance of spotting her. Oh and be sure to bring your boat!
>
> Acorn

**Boat Advert** — On a new day from Summer 11 onwards, at least 2 days after the [Ferry Girl](/tasks/ferry-girl/) quest is done, unless you've already asked Dee about the boat. Sent once.

> Boat for SALE. Works like new. Freshly charmed with high quality leak resistance!! Come see it down at the ferry crossing!

**Book of Fishes Publication** — The morning after you deliver the fish for [A Study in Fish](/tasks/a-study-in-fish/). Sent once.

> We've done it! The book has been published and it looks wonderful. Do come by to collect a copy of your own.
>
> Florian

**Nessa Detention Letter** — After the office reprimand scene in the [Nessa's Curse](/tasks/nessas-curse/) quest. Adds Nessa's detention to the same day.

> Dear,
>
> Dean Clary has informed me of your evening escapade with Miss Nessa Fraying. You will be summoned to Yellow Hall upon waking.
>
> Kind Regards,
>
> Headmistress

**Shed Remodelling letter** — When [Ivarice](/characters/ivarice/) starts the shed upgrades at the end of the garden shed ghosts quest. Arrives the next day.

> Welcome to the Village!
>
> It has come to our attention that you have recently acquired property in the village of Wisk. Practical Practice has proudly supplied villagers with fine furniture and remodelling transformations for centuries.
>
> Stop by and let us transform your home (or garden shed) today!

**Winter Practical Results** — Sent during the winter practical results sequence. Arrives the next day and puts the results class on the calendar for that day.

> Morning!
>
> Your results are in! Come to the Music Hall to receive them.

## Weekly Deliveries (not mail)

- **Seed Collective box**: every Sunday, once you've joined the Seed Collective.
- **Dorm mud pie box**: every Monday, once unlocked.
