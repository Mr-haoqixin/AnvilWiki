## Where do the official Chinese names come from?

Songs of Glimmerwick ships with an official Simplified Chinese localization. This glossary pairs the English names with those official Chinese names across four groups: characters, places, items, and quests. Every Chinese name below was checked against the official in-game Chinese text (data community-extracted from the game files, reviewed October 2026); names that could not be verified are intentionally left out.

Two easy-to-confuse names deserve a note: the item Cauldron is officially 大锅, while the potion-brewing minigame is officially 坩埚（调配）, and the villager Acorn is officially 阿科恩, distinct from the storage item 储物橡果 (Storage Acorn).
