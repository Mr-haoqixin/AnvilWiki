## First Check the Resolutions the Game Offers

Enter the game options and check the resolutions actually listed by the current demo. If 21:9 is offered, check the screen edges, hotbar, and inventory switch keys in a familiar scene, then decide whether to use it.

## Compatible Does Not Mean Full Support

Some players finished playing on ultrawide, and Eastshade also briefly tested successfully; but the developer reported that screen edges may show unfinished areas, and hotbar controls may overlap. Another player still only saw 16:9 options, so do not treat individual success cases as a guarantee of native support for all devices.
