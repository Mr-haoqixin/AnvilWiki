## How do you get around Glimmerwick quickly?

Three systems carry you across the island, in the order you meet them. Walking comes first, and the Swift Witch ability (+2 movement speed, toggleable) is the only speed boost to it. Water crossings come second: five ferry routes and a gondola, joined by your own boat in the summer. The griffin network comes last and beats everything — 11 destinations you can fly between from any griffin bell, each unlocked the first time you visit it. The per-system basics are in the [travel overview](/info/travel-overview/); this page lays out every point, price and quirk from community game-file data, version 1.06.

## How do you buy the boat?

| Detail | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Price | 2,500 gold |
| On sale | Summer 8 (day 21) onward |
| Prerequisite | Complete the Ferry Girl quest |
| How to buy | Talk to Dee, the ferry girl, about the boat |
| Reminder | A Boat Advert letter arrives from day 24 if you have not asked Dee |

Besides open water, the boat pairs with the five ferry routes — each ride lasts 12 seconds, and one of the five runs on the river. The gondola is a cable car between two stations, and your Vested Pack Owl gets a seat too. The quest chain that gates the purchase is walked through in [Get a boat](/tasks/get-a-boat/).

## Does the flying broom actually fly?

No — and that is the joke of it. The flying broom is a Tool-category curio sold by Arthur for 15,000 gold in a conversation, resells for 10,000, and the data is explicit: not used in any recipe, combo or quest, with no movement or travel bonus attached. At 5,000 gold net it is the most expensive bragging right on the island; the [flying broom page](/items/flying-broom/) has the full entry. For real speed, the honest options are Swift Witch for your legs and the griffin for everything else, with the grind to both mapped in [how to unlock every ability](/info/how-to-unlock-every-ability/).

Points, bell locations, prices and durations above all come from community game-file data, version 1.06.
