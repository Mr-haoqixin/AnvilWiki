## Where do crafting blueprints come from?

Four channels supply all 44 recipes. The **Practical Practice** catalog at The Cup & Kettle sells the everyday furniture line, but only on Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Sunday. **Chicory** teaches every pet habitat recipe for free at the Creatures Workshop (Summer Day 3). Two **festival stalls** hold exclusive stock: the Goods Shopkeeper sells the Merfolk series only at the Seaflower Festival, and Gwen sells her stone recipes only at the Pumpkin Moon Festival once Acorn's story opens her shop. The rest arrive through teachers, gifts, a dorm schematic or plain conversation — and a handful have no listed source at all. Carpentry levels 1–4 gate what you can build, and those levels come from the [ability tree](/info/abilities-overview/). Every figure below is community game-file data, version 1.06; the level-by-level view is in the [crafting overview](/info/crafting-overview/).

## Which recipes can you miss forever?

Only the festival stock is time-gated. The Seaflower Festival (Spring 14) is your single shot each year at the Merfolk Bench, Fountain and Lantern blueprints, and the Pumpkin Moon Festival (Fall 13) is the only stall selling Gwen's Stone Bench, Stone Garden Lantern and Stone Trellis — the festival dates and deadlines are in the [festival guide](/info/festival-events-guide/). The Cup & Kettle catalog, by contrast, restocks every Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Sunday, so the weekday rhythm in the [weekday shops schedule](/info/weekday-shops-schedule/) is all you need for the lantern line.

## Do any recipes turn a profit on materials?

Five do at base prices. The **Ornate Trellis** leads at +40 gold (60 of materials selling for 100), the nine level-3 Garden Lantern variants clear +15 each, and the base Garden Lantern, Garden Lantern Cottage and Technical Challenge Trophy squeak by at +5. Everything else sells below its ingredient cost — the Fountain loses 55, every 25-log bench loses 45 — so craft those for your farm or quests like [Garden storage chest](/tasks/garden-storage-chest/), not for the shop counter. The habitat recipes cannot be sold at all; their payoff is in [how to get every pet](/info/how-to-get-every-pet/).

Sources, prices and margins above all come from community game-file data, version 1.06.
