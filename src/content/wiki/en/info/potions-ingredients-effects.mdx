## What does every potion need and do?

Eighteen recipes cover the whole cauldron. Your practice score sets each brew's quality tier — Poor, Average, High, Exceptional or Legendary — and higher tiers sell for more and last longer. The complete table below lists every recipe's ingredients, effect and the score thresholds for all five tiers, with prices measured from community game-file data, version 1.06. The brewing mini-game itself is explained in the [potions overview](/info/potions-overview/).

## Which potions are worth brewing for gold?

Three brewers stand out. **Fisherman's Brew** tops the value table at 282 average and 423 Legendary even though some ingredients never appear in shops — foraged Mermaid's Thread and Seafloor Sand make each cauldron almost pure profit. **Munchitis Treatment** averages 222 off a cheapest-known ingredient cost of just 3 gold (Spring Tubers at 15 each drop the seed back quickly), the widest margin in the file. **Fire Art Potions 1 and 2** average 240 with a buy-in of at least 160, and their recipes arrive free at the Starry Night Celebration.

Two recipes actively destroy gold if you shop for ingredients. **Insta Ungrow** costs 253 to assemble and sells for 60 at Average — a 193-gold loss the data flags directly, because it consumes a brewed Instagrow plus Moon Poppy and salt. **Potion of Speed** needs at least 280 gold of ingredients for a 200-gold Average brew, and **Root Strengthener Tonic** pairs a cost of at least 80 with an Average sell of just 6. Brew those for their effects, not for the shop counter, and pair the rest with the [potion work proficiency](/info/how-to-unlock-every-ability/) abilities that raise your practice score — every threshold you clear is a flat price bump. The economy of what to grow for these cauldrons is ranked in the [crop profit guide](/info/crop-profit-guide/).

## Where does each recipe come from?

Nessa teaches the Fire Art potions and Potion of Speed, Florian the Infernal Snail Repellent, Chrys the Instagrow, and Mel the Potion of Healing; the Fire Art recipes also arrive through the Fire Art Booklet at the Starry Night Celebration. Classes hand out Potion of Hardiness (Potions 101), Insta Ungrow (Potions 102), Spore Neutralizer (Advanced Potions) and Root Strengthener Tonic (winter study session). Books add Munchitis Treatment (Plants of the Western Isles) and Potion of the Mole (Secret Lives of Moles), quests give Befuddlement and Fungal Transmutation, and the remaining four have no listed source. Dates for every class are on the [festival and events guide](/info/festival-events-guide/) and the [weekday shops schedule](/info/weekday-shops-schedule/).

Ingredients, effects, thresholds and prices above all come from community game-file data, version 1.06.
