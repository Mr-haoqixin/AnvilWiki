## Friendship Is Skills

The social system in *Songs of Glimmerwick* is not just a decorative gameplay of giving gifts and grinding affinity, but **directly affects the types of skills you can obtain**. After befriending classmates and townsfolk, they teach unique abilities. The skill range is wide:

- **Practical**: For example, gardening tips that directly boost planting efficiency.
- **Special**: For example, skills like "talking to frogs" that seem niche but can unlock hidden content in specific scenes.

This means "making more friends" is a tangible growth path, not just a story experience.

## Extracurricular Clubs

Extracurricular clubs are where social and gameplay intersect, with each activity bound to a **specific NPC relationship line**:

- **Moth racing team**: Boosts movement efficiency, convenient for daily exploration.
- **Diving**: Obtains rare shell materials, serving crafting and collecting.
- **Forest sprite exploration**: Reaches hidden storylines and secret areas.

Club activities are both a social outlet and a source of bonus buffs and rare resources.

## Character Growth: Skill Tree

Character growth uses a **skill tree system**, allocating development points after leveling up. Since skill sources are tied to NPC relationships, the skill tree direction also reflects the player's social choices — which friends you made and which clubs you joined determine which skill branches open to you. This gives each progression an individualized mark in structure.
