## How do weekdays work in Glimmerwick?

Each of the four seasons lasts exactly 14 days — two clean weeks — and every season starts on a Sunday, so the weekday of any date is fixed: **day 1 and day 8 are Sundays, day 14 is a Saturday**, always. That predictability is the key to the weekly rhythm: classes sit on weekdays (08:00–11:00), festivals land on Fridays and Saturdays, and the rotating shops keep fixed days. The year is 56 days total, and after Winter 14 it rolls into endless spring with no more snow while the calendar keeps counting months. The full dated calendar is in the [calendar overview](/info/calendar-overview/); everything below is the weekday layer, from community game-file data, version 1.06.

## When is each weekly shop open?

**Practical Practice** sets up at The Cup & Kettle on **Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Sunday**, selling the blueprint catalog — Stone Path 25, Cushion 40, Garden Lantern 40 (every variant 40), Carved Bench 50, Chest 100, Trellis 150, Beehive 150, Fountain 80, Lantern 400, Wheatmill 450. Miss those four days and the catalog is simply gone until the next one.

**The Miller** opens **Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday** with a two-item stock: flour for 20 gold and wheat seeds for 20 gold — a cheap, steady baking supply line worth scheduling around if you run the bake stand.

**The Cup & Kettle teahouse** runs evening shows every 10 days starting Summer 1: doors at 17:00, show 18:00–22:00. The Storyteller plays Summer 1, The Wandering Wyrds Summer 11, Galanthus and his Organ Ghouls Fall 7, and Rose and Raven Winter 3.

## Which sellers follow the seasons instead?

The two seed sellers rotate by season rather than weekday: Kavita stocks 32 seasonal seed types each season, and the Waterfall Bay seller behind the Wisk waterfall carries the same 32 at double price out of season — full list in the [crops overview](/info/crops-overview/). Festival stalls are even stricter: Gwen's stone recipes appear only at the Pumpkin Moon Festival and the Merfolk line only at the Seaflower Festival, deadlines covered in the [crafting recipe sources](/info/crafting-recipe-sources/).

## How do you plan a week around all this?

Anchor on Sunday: collect the Seed Collective box and buy any Practical Practice blueprints in one trip, since Sunday is the only day both overlap. Midweek, swing by the Miller on Wednesday's off-day eve — he opens Thursday after a Wednesday closure. Keep Fridays and Saturdays free for festivals, because all five big ones fall on those two days, with the schedule in the [festival guide](/info/festival-events-guide/). Rain and snow also follow the fixed yearly pattern (22 wet days), so a quick look at the [seasonal guide](/info/seasonal-guide/) before planning errands saves a wasted watering trip, and the freeze rules during events are explained in [time mechanics](/info/time-mechanics/).
