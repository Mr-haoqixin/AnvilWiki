## No Confirmed Console Date Yet

As of 2026-10-05, the original page found no confirmed release date for **Nintendo Switch** or **Switch 2**. The Windows PC version has been released, but this does not mean the console version has a confirmed date.

## Distinguish Plans From Official Announcements

Eastshade has talked about console plans, but plans are not the same as announced release dates. The original page also specifically distinguished the misreading of the September 30 date; for the latest status, check official announcements.
