## Who writes to you over the school year?

The Etchery administration is your most devoted correspondent: across the school year it sends the course letters, exam reminders and the Winter Gift Exchange, with individual professors adding their own. Reading a letter is the mechanical trigger — that is what puts its events on your calendar and its entries in your journal, and future-dated letters stay hidden until their morning. Exactly one letter in the whole game hands you a physical item: Florian's Book of Fishes note, which lets you collect a free published copy. Full letter texts are preserved in the [mail overview](/info/mail-overview/); this page tracks the senders and what each letter actually gives you, from community game-file data, version 1.06.

## Do any letters change what you can buy?

Three of them quietly reshape the economy. The Summer Course List unlocks the center door in the Green Hall; The Waterfall Grotto announces an off-season seed seller (seeds there cost double outside their season, per the seed-seller table in the [crops overview](/info/crops-overview/)); and the Boat Advert nudges you toward the 2,500-gold boat that opens five ferry routes, covered in [fast travel and movement](/info/fast-travel-and-movement/). Festival reminder letters also flag the deadlines for the seasonal shop stalls — Gwen sells her stone blueprints only at the Pumpkin Moon Festival, and the Goods Shopkeeper sells the Merfolk furniture line only at the Seaflower Festival.

Sender names, arrival days and unlock effects above all come from community game-file data, version 1.06.
