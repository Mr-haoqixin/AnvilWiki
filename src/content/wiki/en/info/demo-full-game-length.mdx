## Developer-Published Length Estimates

Eastshade said in the June Next Fest announcement that the demo exceeds two hours; the September 25 Steam Deck reply described the full version as about **40 hours**. These are developer estimates, not this wiki's timed results, and do not distinguish main story from full-completion length.

## Download the Free Demo

You can check the free demo entry on the official Steam page. The demo is a sample of the opening content, not the complete paid game; platform and device restrictions should be checked against the current store description.
