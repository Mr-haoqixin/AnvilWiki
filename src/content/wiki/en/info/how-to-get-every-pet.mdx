## How do you actually acquire a pet?

Three steps, identical for every species. First craft and place the species' habitat on your farm. Then find the wild animal and walk up to it while holding a **Gilded Toadstool** — the toadstool is consumed, and it is the bait for all four species. Finally wait one night: your new pet arrives the next day, or waits until you provide a habitat if none is placed. Pets are awake 08:53–18:29 and asleep otherwise, you can keep one of each species, and owning all four earns a Steam achievement. Habitat recipes for the fox, dragon and griffin are taught by Chicory at the Creatures Workshop class on Summer Day 3 — miss it and you will need another way in; only the owl recipe is handed out elsewhere. The mechanics summary lives in the [pets overview](/info/pets-overview/); everything below, from spawn details to costs, is community game-file data, version 1.06.

## Where does each pet spawn?

| Pet | Spawn location | Exact finding spot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Briar Fox | Bellian Moor | 5 possible spots around the moor |
| Woodland Dragon | Ochre Woods | Most often found east of the lake |
| Griffin | Cypress Foothills | Near Marywild's Manor, by the standing water |
| Vested Pack Owl | Nocturne Forest | Anywhere in the forest |

Plan the trip for daytime: a sleeping pet cannot be approached, and 08:53–18:29 is the window when all four are awake.

## What does each habitat cost to build?

| Habitat | Ingredients | Recipe source | Cheapest shop cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Briar Fox Habitat | Twigs×10, Log×10, Stone×10, Seafloor Sand×2 | Chicory, Creatures Workshop | ≥100 (Seafloor Sand not sold) |
| Woodland Dragon Habitat | Twigs×10, Feathered Shroom×2, Porcino×3, Cut Grass×40 | Chicory, Creatures Workshop | ≥20 (mushrooms not sold) |
| Griffin Habitat | Twigs×10, Wild Thyme×5, Thistle×5, Wild Mint×5 | Chicory, Creatures Workshop | ≥20 (herbs not sold) |
| Vested Pack Owl Habitat | Twigs×50, Dread Sorrel×5, Witchfire×5 | Gifted by the fairy royalty leaving the Ochre Woods fairy ring | ≥100 (Dread Sorrel, Witchfire not sold) |

The owl habitat is the stingiest: its recipe only arrives as a parting gift when the fairy royalty leaves the fairy ring, so it is gated on story progress rather than shopping. Ingredient costs use the cheapest shop prices, as in the [crafting recipe sources](/info/crafting-recipe-sources/) — foraged twigs, logs and stone make every habitat nearly free in practice.

## What does each pet do once tamed?

| Pet | Post-tame effect | Why you want it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Briar Fox | Scares vermin away for good — crops can never be eaten again | Removes the nightly vermin chance that gnaws most crops |
| Woodland Dragon | Feed it mushrooms; from the next day up to 7 patches of the fed type grow around its den, each ripening in 2 days | A private mushroom farm |
| Griffin | Pat it and choose Travel to fly to any discovered fast-travel point; makes the Griffon Bells work | Unlocks the whole [fast travel network](/info/fast-travel-and-movement/) |
| Vested Pack Owl | Follows you carrying a 24-slot chest; can be left at and recalled from its habitat | 24 extra inventory slots in the field |

The dragon accepts 11 mushroom types: Chanterelle, Death Cap, Feathered Shroom, Ghost Shroom, Green Shroom, Inky Cap, Morel, Pixie Cup, Porcino, Sunset Amanita and Tundra Cap. Picking one patch starts a new one, so a fed dragon is a perpetual source — handy because Morels go into Instagrow and Ghost Shrooms into the Potion of the Mole, per the [potions overview](/info/potions-overview/).

## Which pet should you get first?

Let your bottleneck decide, because the data draws clear lines. Farming losses point to the **Briar Fox**: it is the only permanent fix for vermin, and most crops carry a nightly 2–8% chance of being eaten. Movement problems point to the **Griffin**, whose 11 fast-travel points cut every errand short. If inventory is the pain, the **owl**'s 24-slot chest dwarfs any other early option. The **dragon** pays slowest but compounds — 7 mushroom patches every 2 days is steady cooking and brewing income, which feeds straight into the [crop profit guide](/info/crop-profit-guide/) economy. For the taming side quest in the moor, see the fox-taming walkthrough in [Bellian Moor fox taming](/tasks/bellian-moor-fox-taming/).
