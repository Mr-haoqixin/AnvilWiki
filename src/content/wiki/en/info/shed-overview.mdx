## Unlock Condition

Remodeling unlocks after the shed ghost story ends: the day after Ivarice starts work, you receive a shed remodeling letter, then use the remodeling catalog in the shed.

### Remodeling Rules

- Each category can switch between options; every changed category costs money
- The first option (original or empty) is always free
- Switching back to a previously purchased style costs again

| Upgrade | Price | Status |
