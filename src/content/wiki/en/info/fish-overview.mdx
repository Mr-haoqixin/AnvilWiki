## Fishing System

**23 fish** total, across 4 water types: ocean, river, lake, mountain stream. Fish bite only during day (06:00–18:00) or evening (18:00–22:00), **unaffected by season or weather**. Each catch costs 16 mana.

### Fishing Process

1. **Cast** — Equip fishing rod, click a fishing spot. Mana must slightly exceed the cost.
2. **Play the fish's tune** — An "!" triggers a rhythm mini-game. Each fish has its own chart, played with left mouse button only.
3. **Score above 85%** — Perfect hit 10 pts, good 8, okay 6; missed or extra press -2.
4. **Pay only on success** — 16 mana deducted only on catch. Failure or cancel costs nothing. Fish have no quality or size.

### Helpful Abilities

- **Song of the Sea**: 100% perfect play catches 2–3 fish at once
- **Treasure Finder**: 5% chance per catch to get 20–50 coins instead
- **Fishmonger**: Fish sell price +20%

## Fishing Buffs

Only two items count as fishing buffs: **Fisherman's Brew** and **Fisherman's Pie**. Buff strength T = quality + 1 (pie counts as quality 1, T=2). Buffs make rare fish more likely and common fish less, and reduce mana cost. Buffs stack.

| Buff | T | Mana per catch | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fisherman's Brew · Poor | 1 | 16 | 60s |
| Fisherman's Brew · Average | 2 | 12.8 | 80s |
| Fisherman's Brew · Good | 3 | 9.6 | 100s |
| Fisherman's Brew · Exceptional | 4 | 6.4 | 120s |
| Fisherman's Brew · Legendary | 5 | 3.2 | 140s |
| Fisherman's Pie | 2 | 12.8 | 80s |

Each quality tier reduces mana cost by 20%; stacked buffs accumulate, minimum 0.

Since v1.04c, the "Fishing Assist" option can add up to 90% of a perfect score to each attempt.

## Fish Trap Song

Fish Trap Refrain places a trap in nearby water (80 mana, 64 after mastery). Return the next day to get one of the following at equal probability (20% each):

- Prismatic Squid
- Saltwater Harp
- Spectral Eel
- Jelly Bell
- Stony Spine

Other catchable items not in the bite table: Blue Trout, Emerald Crab, Stony Spine.

## Trivia

- Some fish list "pond" in their data, but no fishing spot uses pond type — you'll find them in rivers and lakes instead.
- A fishing area named "River" is set to ocean in game data, so it yields ocean fish.
- All fish can be caught in every season, and in year 2+.
- Score only determines catch success — fish have no size or quality.
