## The Role of the Planting System

In *Songs of Glimmerwick*, the planting system is not just life decoration, but the **core pillar of the game economy**. The uses of harvests run through multiple growth lines:

- **Trading for funds**: Sell crops for currency to support other spending.
- **Crafting tools**: Use harvests as materials to craft tools and items.
- **Completing NPC quests**: Many quests require delivering specific crops.

**Botanical Garden** is the player's main workplace, where planting activities are concentrated.

## Three-Stage Progression Route

### Early: Basic Crops and Magic Irrigation

Start with basic crops, focusing on **establishing a magic irrigation flow** — using a rainfall melody to water the fields, linking "farming" and "practicing songs". This stage does not seek high yield, but smooths out the basic loop of planting and musical spellcasting.

### Mid: High-Value Varieties and Composite Melodies

Shift to high-value varieties, and **use composite melodies to optimize growth conditions**. For example, chaining a rainfall melody with a growth-acceleration melody lets one performance meet both water and growth needs at once, significantly boosting output per unit time.

### Late: Rare Varieties and Collection

Enter the stage of cultivating and collecting rare varieties. Rare crops often **require specific seasons and magic combinations** to grow, so late-stage planting is tightly bound to season planning and melody library depth, making it a long-term progression goal.

## The Planting Value of Talking to Trees

The **talk to trees** ability is extremely valuable in planting. Trees can provide:

- **Soil status**: Learn the current land's moisture, fertility, and other info.
- **Hidden planting spots**: Discover special planting locations not visible through normal means.
- **Seasonal advice**: Get hints on what to plant now and when to harvest.

This makes "practicing songs well" not just a need for combat (this game has no combat) or exploration, but the key to planting well.
