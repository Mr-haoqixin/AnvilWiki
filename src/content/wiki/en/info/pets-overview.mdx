## Taming Process

1. **Build a habitat** — Each species needs its own habitat, crafted and placed on your farm. Recipes learned at the Creatures Workshop (Summer Day 3).
2. **Find the wild animal** — Hold a Gilded Toadstool and walk up to it. The toadstool is consumed.
3. **Wait one night** — Taming succeeds! Your new pet arrives the next day. If no habitat is placed, the pet waits until you provide one.

Pets are awake 08:53–18:29, sleeping otherwise. Only one pet per species. Owning all four unlocks a Steam achievement.

## All Pets (4)

| Pet | Location | Habitat Recipe | Post-Tame Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Briar Fox | Berian Wastes | Twigs×10 + Log×10 + Stone×10 + Seafloor Sand×2 | Permanently scares away pests — crops never eaten |
| Dragon | Ochre Woodland (east of lake) | Twigs×10 + Feathered Shroom×2 + Porcino×3 + Cut Grass×40 | Feed mushrooms; up to 7 grow around habitat |
| Griffin | Still water near Mary Wild's estate | Twigs×10 + Wild Thyme×5 + Thistle×5 + Wild Mint×5 | Fly to any discovered fast-travel point |
| Vested Pack Owl | Nocturne Forest | Twigs×50 + Dread Sorrel×5 + Witchfire×5 | Follows you with a 24-slot backpack |

All pets use the same bait: **Gilded Toadstool**.

### Habitat Recipe Sources

- Briar Fox, Dragon, Griffin: Creatures Workshop (Summer Day 3)
- Vested Pack Owl: Gifted when the Fae Royalty leaves the Ochre Woodland fairy ring

### Dragon's Feedable Mushrooms (11)

Feed a mushroom to the dragon; from the next day, up to 7 of that mushroom type grow around the habitat simultaneously. Each takes 2 days to mature; picking one causes a new one to spawn.

Chanterelle, Death Cap, Feathered Shroom, Ghost Shroom, Green Shroom, Inky Cap, Morel, Pixie Cup, Porcino, Sunset Amanita, Tundra Cap
