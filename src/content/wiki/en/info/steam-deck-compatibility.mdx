## Steam Deck Status

The Steam store marks **Songs of Glimmerwick** as **Playable**. This is a platform compatibility classification, not equivalent to every interface, input method, or text size needing no adjustment.

## Before You Start Playing

Check the current controller mapping, whether keyboard input is available, and whether text is easy to read on the small screen. The specific experience may vary with SteamOS, game version, and custom layouts.
