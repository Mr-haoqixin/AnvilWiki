## Four-Season Cycle

*Songs of Glimmerwick* uses a full four-season cycle as its time backbone. Each season brings **different crops, festivals, and events**, so the annual rhythm is itself the core of player planning: what to plant when, and what activities to join when, all need to be scheduled by season.

## Spring: Build the Foundation

Spring is the opening phase, with the goal of **completing core classes to unlock basic magic** and **opening planting areas**. This season gets "learning songs" and "opening land" running, laying the foundation for later high output.

## Summer: Peak Growth Season

Summer is the **peak growth season**. Use long daylight and rain to **accelerate output**, while joining limited-time outdoor activities. This season has the highest planting returns, suitable for rolling out mid-stage high-value varieties.

## Autumn: Concentrated Harvest and Trading

Autumn is the season of **concentrated harvest and trading**. Turn the investment of the previous two seasons into funds and resource reserves, preparing for winter and the next year's opening. This season has the highest density of economic activity.

## Winter: Social and Indoor Activities

Winter suits **social and indoor activities**. Advance NPC relationship lines, join winter festivals, and spend the "can't farm outside" time on making friends and doing indoor quests. Winter is also a good time to round out the skill tree and deepen the story.

## Seasonal Festivals and Limited-Time Rewards

**Seasonal festivals** are the main way to get limited-time rewards. The key rule is: **miss the current season and wait until next year**. So for players who want to collect limited content, it is important to learn each festival's time window and entry conditions in advance, rather than scrambling at the end of the season.
