## Scenes That Pause and Continue Timing

In the public demo, the player menu and shop pause the clock; entering flute mode keeps time going. Before planning a performance, keep an eye on the schedule and current time.

## Use the Music Classroom Practice Room

If you want to stop and practice a song, you can go to the practice room in the music classroom. This note corresponds to the demo context of the original page, and does not guarantee identical time behavior in the full version or later updates.
