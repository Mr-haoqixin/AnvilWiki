## What is the most profitable crop to plant?

Measured as gold per day, the **Pecan tree** wins outright: it first fruits in 8 days, then regrows every single day for 65 gold each harvest, and its seed cannot even be bought — you plant what you forage. Among seeds sold in shops, **Cocoa Bean** is the champion at 50 gold per day (100-gold harvests every 2 days after a 6-day start), and **Cucumber** is the best budget entry at 30 gold per day from a 30-gold seed that pays for itself on day 3. Every figure in the ranking below comes from community game-file data, version 1.06.

## How is gold per day calculated?

For one-harvest plants the number is (sell price × yield − cheapest seed price) ÷ days to harvest, assuming you replant immediately. For regrowing crops and trees it is the sell price ÷ days between harvests once the plant is established. The math uses base sell prices with daily watering and ignores seed drops, vermin losses and the Farm Merchant ability's 10% crop bonus, so real runs usually earn slightly more. Flower Farmer, a hidden ability from the shed ghost story, matures every flower one night early — that is why flowers list two day counts above.

Two rules from the data keep this table simple. Crops have no growing season, so nothing stops you from planting Pecan in Winter; seasons only decide which seeds shops stock, as the [crops overview](/info/crops-overview/) explains. And the weather is fixed every year — 22 rain and snow days auto-water for you, dates listed in the [calendar overview](/info/calendar-overview/).

## Which plants actively lose money?

Three entries have negative gold per day when grown from bought seeds: **Lakedew Lily** (-165 per day on paper — a 500-gold seed that sells for 5, clearly grown for a quest, not profit), **Twilight Blossom** (-13.3), and **Wild Wheat** (-3.3). Treat Colossal Pumpkin separately too: its 45-gold entry never comes to market because it keeps growing into a beanstalk instead of maturing — see how in the [gardening guide](/info/gardening-guide/). For filling the rest of your gold pouch between harvests, the [best fish for money](/info/best-fish-to-catch-for-money/) ranking is the natural companion.

Growth days, sell prices, seed prices and pay-off days above are all from community game-file data, version 1.06.
