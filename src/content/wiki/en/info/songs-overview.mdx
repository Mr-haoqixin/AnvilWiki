## Song System

22 songs total: 18 spell songs + 4 performance pieces. Enter flute mode and play the note sequence to cast. Only note order matters, not rhythm.

Three levels:
1. **Acquired**: Know the melody, play it to cast
2. **Learned**: Reach learn score in music room, enables quick-cast, +1 XP
3. **Perfected**: Reach perfect score, each cast costs 20% less mana, +1 XP

## Unpracticable Songs

These 5 songs stay at "Acquired" and can only be cast by playing the melody — they cannot be practiced in the music room:

- Lakedew Lily Song
- Song of Silence
- Tangleknot Remover
- Alchemical Resonance
- Ballad of Growth

## Music Room & Practice

Practice by playing the flute in one of three ancient music rooms. Each note: Perfect +10, Good +8, Sloppy +6; missing or wrong key: -2 points.

- Learn score = notes × 6
- Perfect score = notes × 10 × 0.9
- Accessibility option "Song Mastery Assist" lowers both scores
