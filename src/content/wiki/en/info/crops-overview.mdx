## Farming System

**58 plants** total: 43 crops + 9 flowers + 5 trees + 1 pond plant. 27 regrow after harvest. 36 seeds are sold in shops.

**Crops have no growing season** — you can plant anything at any time. Seasons only change which seeds shops sell.

### Farming Basics

- **Water daily**: Watering sets tile moisture to 3, decreasing by 1 each night. Plants only grow on nights with moisture ≥ 2. Trees only need watering until the first growth stage.
- **Rain/snow auto-waters**: Weather is fixed each year. 22 rain/snow days: Spring 5/9/12, Summer 4/12, Autumn 2/4/6/7/8/11/12, Winter 1/2/3/4/5/7/8/9/10/12.
- **Season change doesn't kill crops**: The game never checks season during crop growth.
- **Pests**: Some crops have a small nightly chance of being eaten. Taming a Briar Fox permanently removes pests.

### Helpful Abilities

- **Flower Farmer** (hidden): Flower crops mature 1 day early
- **Seed Gatherer**: 1.5× seed drop rate on harvest
- **Farm Merchant**: Crop sell price +10%

Colossal Pumpkin is planted in a tree tile. It never matures — keep watering and it grows each night, eventually becoming a giant beanstalk.

## Special Plants

- **Cranberry**: Planted in pond water, matures in 3 nights without watering, scythe harvest yields 2 berries, 25% seed chance
- **Sprinkler Plant**: Not a crop — watering it waters all surrounding tiles, chaining to other sprinkler plants. Purchase price 120
- **Lakedew Lily**: Seed costs 500 gold, sells for only 5 gold (loss-making crop, likely quest-related)
