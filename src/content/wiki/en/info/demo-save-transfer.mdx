## Check the Continue Menu First

Eastshade once promised that demo progress could continue. Launch-day player reports were inconsistent: some saw demo saves in **Continue**, some did not.

## Avoid Overwriting the Original Save

First confirm the current Windows user, game store, and in-game save list. Do not immediately move or overwrite save files just because they do not appear in Continue yet.
