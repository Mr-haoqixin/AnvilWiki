## Official Feature List

The official press release and reviews for *Songs of Glimmerwick* list extracurriculars including: **forming a band, diving for shells, forest exploration**, as well as exploring islands, joining school clubs, and chatting with classmates. These activities are not optional decoration, but real gameplay that provides **bonus buffs and rare resources**.

## Fishing: Music Integration

Fishing is deeply integrated with musical spellcasting: **each fish has a unique melody**. This means fishing is not just "waiting for a bite", but requires you to listen and play the corresponding melody — the stronger your musical ability, the smoother fishing goes. This design makes fishing an extension of the music system rather than a standalone mini-game.

## Diving for Shells

Diving is used to **obtain rare shell materials**. Shells are an important source for crafting and collecting, so diving serves the broader progression loop.

## Forest Exploration

Forest exploration lets you **join Green Sprites on forest expeditions**, reaching hidden storylines and secret areas. This is a branch of exploration gameplay leaning toward "discovering secrets", complementing daily planting and fishing in rhythm.

## Festivals

**Seasonal festivals** are the main way to get limited-time rewards. Festival content changes with the season, and missing the current season means waiting until next year, so festival time windows need to be planned in advance.

## Exploration and Clubs

More broadly, exploring islands, joining school clubs, and chatting with classmates all fall under extracurriculars. Together they form the main content for "not in class, not farming" time, and intertwine the three threads of music, socializing, and exploration.
