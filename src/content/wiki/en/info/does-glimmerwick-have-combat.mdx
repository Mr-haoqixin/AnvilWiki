## No Traditional Combat

Eastshade's direct answer is: **Songs of Glimmerwick has no combat**. Magic songs mainly serve exploration, puzzle-solving, and daily tasks, rather than combat skills.

## Main Gameplay

Players can attend classes, practice musical magic, plant and explore islands, and advance story quests on campus. The original page lists these activities as alternative experiences to combat gameplay.
