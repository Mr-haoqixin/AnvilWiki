## What does it take to unlock an ability?

One skill point per hexagon, and one point for every 11 XP — the curve never steepens, so each level is worth exactly one ability. Twenty-four hexagons follow the chain rule: a hexagon becomes unlockable the moment **any connected ability** is already unlocked, which is why the order is yours to choose. Three more are free from the start, and the remaining four hide as unknown hexagons until specific story scenes reveal them. Collecting all 31 takes 341 XP, level 32. Max mana starts at 350 and each Mana Enrichment adds 100, capped at 1,000. The full effects table by tier is in the [ability tree overview](/info/abilities-overview/); this page is the unlock playbook, from community game-file data, version 1.06.

## How do the four hidden abilities get revealed?

Hidden abilities sit on the tree as unknown hexagons you can pay for blind — or you can wait for the reveal:

| Ability | Effect | Revealed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flower Farmer | Flower crops ripen one night sooner | The garden shed ghosts (Marywild) storyline |
| Mole Strength | Inventory grows from 30 to 40 slots | The Giant Mole, after Worms for Moles |
| Apothecary | 35% chance to brew two potions instead of one | The Potionmaster, during her ingredients quest |
| Wind Wielder | Rain clouds and whirlwinds drift twice as fast | The Cloud Shepherd on the beanstalk |

Each reveal is story-gated, so a maxed tree is also a finished story: the shed remodel arc in the [shed upgrade guide](/info/shed-upgrade-guide/), the mole quest line, the Potionmaster's errand, and the beanstalk endgame.

## Where does the XP come from?

| Source | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Complete a quest (minor / major) | 3 / 5 |
| Learn or perfect a song (first time each) | 1 |
| New potion quality tier | 1 |
| Restore an ancient shrine | 2 |
| Summer Music Festival, mastered on site | 5 |
| Summer Music Festival, otherwise | 3 |
| Camping trip (release the Bloom flower) | 3 |
| Bathhouse | 1 per day |
| First Sandcastle Jingle at each sand pile | 1 |
| Cloud Shepherd on the beanstalk | 4 |

Stack the daily and repeatable sources: the bathhouse pays 1 XP every day and Sandcastle Jingles pay once per pile, which turns idle mornings into ability points. The festival performance is the single biggest award at 5 XP — the win conditions are in the [festival guide](/info/festival-events-guide/).

## Is there a 32nd ability?

In the files, yes — **Moon Channeler** exists but its hexagon is disabled, so it cannot be obtained in any version of normal play. Two more exist only in code, equally unreachable: **Logger** (30% chance of 4× logs) and **Miner** (30% chance of 2× stone). The practical tree is the 31 above, and unlocking all of them earns a Steam achievement tracked in the [achievements overview](/achievements/achievements-overview/).

Unlock rules, effects, reveal scenes and XP values above all come from community game-file data, version 1.06.
