## When is each festival held?

Seven festival-type events pace the school year, one roughly every season plus the class camping trip. Time freezes at each festival's start hour until you walk in, so you can finish chores first. Every date and win threshold below is from community game-file data, version 1.06.

## How do you win the Seaflower Festival?

Two competitions decide it. **Flower judging**: fill the Flower Chest with more than 20 flowers in total — stacked blooms all count, so mass beats rarity. **Tidepool diving**, the Merfolk judge's challenge: dive the zones and score more than 8 points before a 60-second countdown runs out. Winning earns the Conch instrument, the Merfolk Outfit and a Steam achievement, and everyone who arrives receives a Wetsuit Outfit and learns the Sandcastle Jingle song. A reminder letter from Professor Rohesia arrives on Spring 13, and leaving the festival completes the Herbotany Homework quest. Note for shoppers: the festival also hosts the Goods Shopkeeper stall selling the Merfolk furniture blueprints, listed in the [crafting recipe sources](/info/crafting-recipe-sources/).

## What should you bring to the Summer Music Festival?

Your flute, practiced. Performing the festival song on site grants 5 XP if you master it straight away and 3 XP otherwise, and the performance hands you the Fall Fest Outfit — some rewards depend on your minigame result. You receive a Summer Flower Crown when the festival starts, and an instrument vendor sells the Lyre for 200 gold; visit the music box vendor before the festival can end. The preparation letter from Professor Linnea lands on Summer 13 itself.

## What does the Pumpkin Moon Festival judge?

Your colossal pumpkin and your throwing arm. Pumpkin collection starts the day before the festival, and on festival morning the pumpkin's growth is judged — **growth above 18 wins** the competition. The apple toss gives you 10 apples to aim and power-throw at targets worth 0, 2, 3, 5 or 10 points, and **scoring more than 38 wins**. Wins pay out the Gourd Guitar instrument and the Purple Winter Cloak outfit plus an achievement. Gwen's stall at the festival sells her stone recipes, and the Practical Practice stall stocks festival candies — the pumpkin-growing side of things is covered in the [gardening guide](/info/gardening-guide/).

## What do you do at the Starry Night Celebration?

Three things, in any order: hunt, gift, and learn. **Eight red ribbons** are hidden around the festival and finding at least five wins the hunt. The gift exchange asks you to bring a toy for your assigned child — the Winter 5 letter names Theo, and a toymaker stays in the town market all season. Reading the Fire Art Booklet on site teaches both Fire Art potion recipes. The reward slate runs from those recipes and a Fire Art Potion 1 to Cider, Theo's Hat Gift, the Kalimba instrument for winners, and a Steam achievement; the sleigh leaves the Etchery courtyard at 17:00, full details in [Starry Night Gifting](/tasks/starry-night-gifting/).

## Is the camping trip worth the two days?

Yes, because it is low-effort. The Summer 7 trip to Bellian Moor auto-waters your plants while you are away, and releasing the Bloom flower during the trip is worth 3 XP — one of the larger single XP awards outside main quests, useful for the grind described in [how to unlock every ability](/info/how-to-unlock-every-ability/). Only seven characters attend, making it also the quietest festival socially.
