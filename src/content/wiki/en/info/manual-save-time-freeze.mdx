## Full Version Manual Saves

Launch-week player replies said that saving is possible without sleeping first. If the current menu offers **Save**, execute it and go to the **Load** page to confirm the save slot actually appears. This is a player report; the original page did not claim to have tested all cutscenes or active scenes.

## Distinguish From Old Demo Reports

The reminder about being unable to save during nighttime time freeze comes from an earlier demo, and does not mean all full-version scenes are under the same restriction. If Save is unavailable, first confirm whether the current event allows saving, and do not overwrite your only save.
