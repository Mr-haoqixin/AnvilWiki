## Game Basics

*Songs of Glimmerwick* is developed and published by **Eastshade Studios**. The studio previously also developed the adventure game *Eastshade*. The game is set in a magic academy and island daily life, blending open-world exploration, role-playing, and life simulation elements, and supports single-player play.

The PC version was released on **Steam** on **September 30, 2026**. Pre-release coverage mentioned console versions would follow; the exact release status and dates for console platforms should be based on official follow-up announcements, and "planned to release" should not be treated as already on the market.

![Waterfall and woodland scenery of Glimmerwick Island (original wiki image)](https://glimmerwick.wiki/media/waterfall-1280.webp)

## Academy Life and Musical Spellcasting

The player takes the role of a magic academy student, experiencing classes and daily life through the changing of four seasons. Magic centers on music: the player plays notes to form melodies, turning songs into spells; different songs can assist with daily activities such as fishing, gardening, and exploration.

Exploring the world and completing tasks earns experience and unlocks practical skills. The player can also plant in the garden or collect materials, then use them to brew potions. The gameplay loop is therefore not just attending classes and doing quests, but also includes exploration, gathering, planting, and crafting.
