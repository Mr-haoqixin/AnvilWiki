## Griffin Fast Travel (11 points)

Fast travel requires a tamed griffin. Pet it (travel) or ring a griffin bell to open the map. Destinations unlock when first visited.

| --- | --- |

| 11 | Mary Wild's Estate |

## Griffin Bells (11)

Bells across the island summon your griffin and open the travel map. Without a griffin, bells just ring once. Each bell is near a fast-travel point.

## Boat

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Price | 2,500 gold |
| Available | Summer Day 8 (day 21) |
| Purchase | Talk to ferry girl Dee about the boat |
| Prerequisite | Complete quest "Ferry Girl" |

If you haven't asked Dee about the boat, a boat advert letter arrives from day 24.

## Ferries & Gondola

- **Ferries**: 5 water ferry routes (1 on a river), 12 seconds each
- **Gondola**: Cable car between two stations; your Vested Pack Owl can ride too
