## Ability Tree System

**31 abilities** total, each costing 1 skill point. Every 11 XP grants 1 level and 1 skill point — the curve never changes. Unlocking all 31 abilities requires 341 XP (level 32).

Carpentry, Potion Work Proficiency, and Mana Enrichment each have three independent hexagons (tiers I–III), each costing 1 point.

4 **hidden abilities** appear as unknown hexagons until revealed: Flower Farmer, Mole Strength, Apothecary, Wind Wielder.

2 abilities are **broken in v1.06** (the game never checks them): Spirit of Flour, Blessing of the Merfolk.

2 **toggle abilities** (click the hexagon in-game to toggle on/off): Light Bearer, Swift Witch.

## Notes & Oddities

- Max mana starts at 350; each Mana Enrichment adds +100 (cap 1,000).
- **Blessing of the Merfolk** and **Spirit of Flour** consume skill points but have no effect in v1.06.
- **Moonbringer** exists in game files but its hexagon is disabled — unobtainable.
- Two more abilities exist only in code, unobtainable through normal play: **Lumberjack** (30% chance 4× wood) and **Miner** (30% chance 2× stone).
- Unlocking all abilities in the tree grants a Steam achievement.
