## Role in the Prologue

The launch-day prologue guide identifies Bristlecone as the flute maker. His shop is inside a felled tree; players bring [Abigailia](/characters/abigailia/)'s letter to speak with him.

## How to Obtain the Flute

Bristlecone first sends players to [Wisk](/locations/wisk/) to collect other school supplies. Once all items on the list are gathered, return to his shop to claim the [flute](/items/flute/); if the flute is still not obtained, confirm whether the return visit step has been completed.

For the full route, see the [First Day of School Supplies guide](/tasks/first-day-school-supplies/).
