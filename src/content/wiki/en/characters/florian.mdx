## Friendship Line and Bookstore

The reference site guide lists Florian's book line as one of the priority friendship lines. Florian runs the bookstore, which is mentioned in version updates. Along with [Clover](/characters/clover/) and [Mel](/characters/mel/), he is a priority friendship line.

## Fish Delivery Quest

The guide records that players can deliver fish to Florian for rewards; see [Deliver Fish to Florian](/tasks/florian-fish-chest/).

## Version Fix

The [Version 1.04b update notes](/updates/version-1-04b/) record a fix for players being blocked out of Florian's bookstore.
