## Role in the Prologue

The launch-day prologue guide identifies Abigailia as the headmistress of Etchery University. After arriving on the island by boat, players follow the ascending path to speak with her and receive a letter, then head to [Wisk](/locations/wisk/) to collect supplies.

## Her Role in the Enrollment Route

The guide records that Abigailia directs players south to find the flute maker [Bristlecone](/characters/bristlecone/). If the path is still blocked by trees, complete the conversation with her first; the guide says she clears the route.

To view supply collection and the subsequent dorm sequence, see the [First Day of School Supplies guide](/tasks/first-day-school-supplies/).
