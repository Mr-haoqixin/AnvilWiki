## Role in the Prologue

The launch-day prologue guide identifies Heather as the dorm advisor. After completing the enrollment ceremony where [Abigailia](/characters/abigailia/) welcomes new students, players follow other students into the dorm and speak with her. This sequence follows the supply collection in [Wisk](/locations/wisk/).

## First Night Flow

The guide records that after speaking, players should head to the assigned room on the lower right and rest on the bed to end the first day. If the flow does not advance, follow the source hint to confirm whether the conversation with Heather is complete and the room entered.

This sequence follows the [school supply collection](/tasks/first-day-school-supplies/).
