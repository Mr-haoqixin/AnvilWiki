## Quest Association

The reference site guide records that Clover is one of the named townsfolk in the quest network, associated with the tulip and wool quest. The Business Buds achievement requires gathering enough tulips for Clover; the launch-week unlock rate is about 25%, indicating most players encounter this quest line in the first week.

## Quest Trigger

The guide suggests talking to everyone in the first game week—quests like tulip and wool only trigger from dialogue. Clover's quest line is one of the recommended priorities in the "advance one friendship line at a time" strategy.

For related quest and event investigation, see [Missable Events and Time](/tasks/missable-events-time/). Clover, [Mel](/characters/mel/), and [Florian](/characters/florian/) are all priority friendship lines; completing them sequentially is recommended.
