## Friendship Line and Curse

The reference site guide lists Nessa's curse as one of the priority friendship lines. The Inquiry Grotto and Nessa's curse have a launch-week unlock rate of about 3%, indicating most players are still exploring.

## Related Quests

Nessa's associated clues are at [Bonesand Cherry Location](/tasks/bonesand-cherry-location/). Version 1.01 fixed a bathhouse softlock during Nessa's vigil; see [Version 1.01 Update Notes](/updates/version-1-01/). Nessa and [Mel](/characters/mel/) are both priority friendship lines.
