## Music History Class and the Silence

Professor Linnea teaches Music History at [Etchery University](/locations/etchery-university/). The course covers how witches after the Silence formed a cult to resist it. The cult secretly kept music to hide from the universe, later developing into Etchery.

## Pickaxe Spell

Linnea teaches the Pickaxe spell in class, used to remove boulders in the [Botanical Garden](/locations/botanical-garden/). This is one of the key spells obtained in the prologue; see [Unlock Pickaxe Spell and Garden Boulders](/tasks/unlock-pickaxe-garden-boulders/).

## Spell Scale

The course reveals over 300 million learnable spells, of which over 20,000 are for changing weather. This setting provides a vast spell space for subsequent exploration.
