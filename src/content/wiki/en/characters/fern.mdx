## Ginger Calming Tea Trade

Fern's tea quest requires giving the Instagrow potion to Chrys in exchange for Ginger Calming Tea. After completion, return to Fern to deliver it. See [Fern's Quests: Ginger Calming Tea and Confrontation](/tasks/fern-quests/).

## Confrontation After Strange Pamphlets

Fern's confrontation is the main quest after Strange Pamphlets. The silent printing press produces Cacophony fragments; give them to Fern for 100 Gold Petals, or give them to Headmistress for 300 then speak with Fern. Both routes can start Fern's confrontation.

## Northern Grounds Meeting

Meet Fern at the Northern Grounds entrance and follow her to Green Sprites Headquarters. These are sourced route choices, not claims that rewards can be repeatedly collected.
