## Mud Pie Delivery Quest

The guide records that players can deliver mud pies to Theo. Version 1.04b fixed an issue where delivering to him was hard to trigger; see [Version 1.04b Update Notes](/updates/version-1-04b/). Mud pies are a course product; the related academy is [Etchery University](/locations/etchery-university/). For the Version 1.01 fix, see [Version 1.01 Update Notes](/updates/version-1-01/).
