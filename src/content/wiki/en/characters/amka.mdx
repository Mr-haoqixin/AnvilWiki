## Unlocking the Bake Stand

Amka visits the [Botanical Garden](/locations/botanical-garden/) and proposes setting up a bake stand. After accepting her proposal, the stand appears across from the magic well after two mornings. See [Garden stand not responding? Unlock and use Amka's bake stand](/tasks/garden-bake-stand/).

## Selling Homemade Food

The stand is used to sell homemade food, including baked goods and pickles. Place homemade food on the stand to collect income overnight. The guide reports a 20% stand bonus. The selling operation is similar to [selling items at the garden well](/tasks/sell-items-gold-petals/).

## Store-Bought Food Excluded

Store-bought food is excluded and cannot be sold through the stand. The guide warns that merging homemade food into bought food stacks may cause it to be counted as purchased, and recommends keeping homemade food in a separate stack.
