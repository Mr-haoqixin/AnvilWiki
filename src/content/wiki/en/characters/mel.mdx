## Friendship Line

The reference site guide lists Mel's mending as one of the priority friendship lines (alongside [Clover](/characters/clover/), [Florian](/characters/florian/), and [Nessa](/characters/nessa/)). Advancing one line at a time and completing sequentially is recommended.
