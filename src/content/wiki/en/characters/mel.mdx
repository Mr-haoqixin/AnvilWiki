## Friendship Line

The reference site guide lists Mel's mending as one of the priority friendship lines (alongside [Clover](/characters/clover/), [Florian](/characters/florian/), and [Nessa](/characters/nessa/)). Advancing one line at a time and completing sequentially is recommended.

## Related Locations and Quests

Mel's associated clues are spread across multiple quest pages: the seafloor sand diving spot is at [Seafloor Sand Diving Spot](/tasks/seafloor-sand-diving/), and the lighthouse keeper clue is at [Lighthouse Keeper and the Darkest Sea](/tasks/lighthouse-keeper-darkest-sea/).
