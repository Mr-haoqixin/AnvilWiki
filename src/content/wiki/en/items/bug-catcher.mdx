## What is the bug catcher?

Spiders for Peony is the moment most players go looking for this net. The bug catcher is one of the five Tool-category items, described in the game files with two flat words: "Catch bugs." Community game-file data (version 1.06) lists no recipe, combo, or quest that consumes it, but catching the five spiders that [Peony's Spiders](/tasks/peonys-spiders/) asks for is done with this item — the details are in [Catch Spiders](/tasks/catch-spiders-bug-catcher/).

## How do you get the bug catcher?

Three routes exist, and two of them are free. [Kavita](/characters/kavita/) sells it for 55 coins in every season; it is also a reward from [The Beekeeper's Bees](/tasks/the-beekeepers-bees/) quest; and the data lists it as an item handed over during a villager conversation. Since the shop price and the sell price are both 55, buying it early is zero-risk — you can always claw the money back.

## What is the bug catcher used for?

Catching bugs and spiders, and nothing else in the data: no recipe, combo, or quest consumes the tool itself. The bugs you catch matter, though — spiders feed Peony's delivery quest, and the tool is effectively the gate on that friendship favor. Beyond that, the data draws a blank, so do not expect crafting surprises later.

## Bug catcher or scythe first?

If both are on your shopping list, the answer depends on your season. The scythe at 65 coins feeds the ingredient economy every single day, while the bug catcher only pays off when a bug-related quest is active. Buy the [scythe](/items/scythe/) first in spring, and grab the bug catcher whenever the Peony or beekeeper quest appears — or skip the purchase entirely if a free source has already landed it in your inventory.
