## Where to Obtain

The launch-day prologue walkthrough lists the school robe among the first-day school supplies and directs players to pick it up at [Wisk](/locations/wisk/)'s [Swish and Stitch Clothier](/locations/swish-and-stitch-clothier/).

Existing sources do not state whether the robe grants stat bonuses, whether it can be swapped, or whether other styles exist; these details are not inferred for now.

For the full collection route, see [First Day School Supplies Walkthrough](/tasks/first-day-school-supplies/).
