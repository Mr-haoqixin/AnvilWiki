## What is the fishing rod?

The fishing rod is one of the five Tool-category items in Songs of Glimmerwick and the only way to catch the fish that fill the game's rivers, lakes, ponds, and ocean. It does not stack, and the game files give it the flavor line "Hook free!" — a small joke, since the rod costs a fair chunk of early-game money. According to community game-file data (version 1.06), it is also a required item for the fish encyclopedia quest [A Study in Fish](/tasks/a-study-in-fish/).

## Where do you buy the fishing rod?

Three sellers stock it for 200 coins in every season: [Lusina](/characters/lusina/), [Mel](/characters/mel/), and the Merfolk fish stall at Merfolk beach. There is no crafting recipe, no forage spot, and no quest that hands it over, so saving up the purchase price is the only route. If fishing itself is the confusing part, the step-by-step guide is at [How to Fish](/tasks/fishing-rod-how-to-fish/).

## What is the fishing rod used for?

Its single listed quest use is A Study in Fish, Florian's encyclopedia request — the community data marks the rod as a required item for that quest line. Day to day, it is your access point to every fish in the [fish overview](/info/fish-overview/), which in turn feeds recipes like Uncooked Fisherman's Pie and the Published Author achievement. The data lists no recipe or combo that consumes the rod itself, so once bought it never leaves your toolkit.

## Is the fishing rod worth 200 coins?

Yes, and earlier is better. Fishing supplies free ingredients for cooking and quest deliveries, the sellback price of 100 means half the cost is recoverable in a pinch, and Florian's quest chain is one of the friendship lines the game expects you to finish. Buy it the moment Lusina or Mel opens for business rather than hoarding petals.
