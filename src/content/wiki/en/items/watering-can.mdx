## What is the watering can?

At 5 coins, this is one of the cheapest purchases in the game and one of the first tools you will ever hold. In the item database the watering can is filed under Ingredient rather than Tool, because the crafting system treats it as a material: dough is made from Flour and a watering can, with the near-identical waterskin as an alternative input. Community game-file data (version 1.06) gives it the tagline "Keep the garden growing!"

## Where do you get a watering can?

[Kavita](/characters/kavita/) sells it for 5 coins in every season, and the data additionally lists 2 forage spots where one can be picked up. Between those two sources you should never be without one — and if it goes missing, the troubleshooting page [Watering Can Not Found](/tasks/lost-watering-can/) documents where it tends to hide, usually behind scenery near the pond or garden well.

## What is the watering can used for?

Two jobs, one mundane and one mildly absurd. Mundane: watering tilled crops so seeds take and grow, the loop described in [Plant Seeds After Tilling](/tasks/plant-seeds-after-tilling/). Absurd: the crafting system counts it as a dough ingredient — dough requires Flour plus a watering can or waterskin, feeding every bread and pastry in the [crafting overview](/info/crafting-overview/). The game clearly knows this is a joke; no other tool ends up kneaded into bread.

## Watering can or waterskin for dough?

Either works, and both cost 5 coins from Kavita, so the choice is cosmetic. The differences in the data are tiny: the waterskin has no forage spots, while the watering can has 2, and their taglines differ ("Filtered for drinking" versus "Keep the garden growing!"). If you are buying in bulk for a baking session, grab whichever is closer in the shop menu — the recipe accepts both.
