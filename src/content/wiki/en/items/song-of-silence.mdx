## Mill Performance

The walkthrough records that Song of Silence is used for mill-performance spellcasting; see [Cast Song of Silence](/tasks/cast-song-of-silence/).

## Song Mechanics

The reference-site walkthrough notes that each song corresponds to a world effect; songs are keys, not weapons. Learning a song is a flute-rhythm mini-game; mastering it makes the effect faster and more stable. See [Song Mastery Tips](/tasks/song-mastery-help/). Performance requires a [Flute](/items/flute/).
