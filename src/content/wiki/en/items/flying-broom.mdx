## What is the flying broom?

Arthur keeps the most expensive tool in the game behind a conversation option: a flying broom described in the item files as "a rare curio from the Eastern Isles." It sits in the Tool category alongside the [fishing rod](/items/fishing-rod/) and the [scythe](/items/scythe/), but unlike those it is a luxury purchase, not a progression gate. Community game-file data (version 1.06) attaches no recipe, combo, or quest to it, so treat it as a collector piece for witches with far too many [gold petals](/items/gold-petals/).

## How do you get the flying broom?

You buy it from [Arthur](/characters/arthur/) — not from a shop menu, but during a normal conversation — for 15,000 coins. No crafting recipe, quest reward, or forage spot exists for it, and the data lists no seasonal restriction on the purchase. At 15,000 coins it costs 150 times the price of a watering can, which makes it a deliberate endgame gold sink rather than an early must-have.

## What does the flying broom actually do?

Nothing mechanical, according to the data: it is "not used in any recipe, combo or quest," and no movement or travel bonus is attached to the item entry. If you are shopping for faster movement instead of trophies, the practical options are covered in the [travel overview](/info/travel-overview/), from unlocked paths to the boat. The broom's real function is bragging rights and the sellback option.

## Is it worth 15,000 coins?

Only after everything else. That sum covers most of a year's seeds, every tool in the Tool category many times over, or a large slice of the 100,000 petals needed for the Seasons of Plenty achievement. Selling it back recovers 10,000 coins, so the net cost of showing it off is 5,000 — a fair price for the one item in the game that signals a finished wallet.
