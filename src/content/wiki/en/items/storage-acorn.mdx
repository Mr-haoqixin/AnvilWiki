## What is the storage acorn?

Despite the name, no storage function is attached to this item anywhere in the game files. The storage acorn sits in the Miscellaneous category with the item tagline "Gift from the Trees," and community game-file data (version 1.06) lists no shop, quest, crafting, or foraging source for it — nor any recipe, combo, or quest that uses it. It is best understood as a rare trophy from the crow delivery system rather than an upgrade item.

## How do you get a storage acorn?

The only route in the data is the Friend of Crows ability. That chain ability makes a crow drop a random item near your farm each morning — see the [abilities overview](/info/abilities-overview/) — and the community data includes the storage acorn among the possible drops, with a fraction-of-a-percent daily chance. In other words: unlock the ability, keep farming, and treat any acorn as a lucky morning, never a goal.

## Does the storage acorn expand your inventory?

No. Actual storage comes from elsewhere: the garden storage chest and the extra bag slots tied to the giant mole are covered in [Garden Storage Chest](/tasks/garden-storage-chest/). If you came here looking for more inventory space, that page — not this acorn — is the answer. The name invites the assumption, and the data simply does not support it.

## Is it worth keeping or selling?

Neither is possible in the data: no sell price is listed and no vendor asks for it. Keep it as a curiosity, the same way players keep the gross garment or the music box — items whose whole content is the finding. For everything that does have a price and a purpose, the [items directory](/items/items-directory/) indexes all 552 entries.
