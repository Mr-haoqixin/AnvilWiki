## What are shears?

Unlike the shop-sold tools, shears never appear on a store shelf — you make them yourself. The item sits in the Crafted category rather than the Tool category, and its in-game description explains the perk: it "gives chance of collecting seeds while wildcrafting (does not need to be equipped)." Community game-file data (version 1.06) lists no recipe, combo, or quest that consumes the shears; their value is the passive seed bonus and the sheep-shearing interaction described in [Sheep Shearing](/tasks/unlock-shears-shear-sheep/).

## How do you unlock and craft shears?

The recipe is taught by [Professor Rohesia](/characters/professor-rohesia/) — per the launch-day guide, during the summer camping trip, so the recipe arrives with the season rather than with a shop visit. Once learned, the crafting recipe is:

| Ingredient | Quantity | Shop price if bought |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Iron Bar | 2 | 15 coins each from Hyla |
| Stone | 5 | 4 coins each |

Bought outright, the ingredients run 50 coins against a 45-coin sell value, so crafting your own is the only sane path. With the Friend of Crows ability from the [abilities overview](/info/abilities-overview/), crows can also drop shears as one of their random morning gifts — a lucky shortcut, never a plan.

## What are shears used for?

Two things. First, the passive effect: while wildcrafting (gathering wild plants), shears grant a chance to collect seeds from what you harvest, quietly cutting your seed budget at [Kavita](/characters/kavita/)'s stall. Second, sheep: clicking a sheep in Rosewheat Fields with the wrong tool does nothing, and the shears are the intended answer, which matters when [Clover's Cozy Scarves](/tasks/clovers-cozy-scarves/) asks for 12 wool.

## Do shears need to be equipped?

No — the item description states the bonus works without equipping them, which is unusual for a tool. Keep them in storage and every wildcrafting session still rolls for bonus seeds. That also means there is no reason to carry them in a hotbar slot once crafted.
