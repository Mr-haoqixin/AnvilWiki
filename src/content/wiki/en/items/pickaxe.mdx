## Can you get the pickaxe in Songs of Glimmerwick?

No — and this is a data answer, not a guess. The pickaxe sits in the game files as one of the five Tool-category items with the description "Good for breaking up stone," but community game-file data (version 1.06) shows no way to get it in normal play: no shop stocks it, no quest rewards it, no crafting recipe builds it, and no forage spot hides it. The data also gives it a base value of 200 coins and marks it unsellable, which is the profile of a leftover or disabled item rather than a hidden unlock.

## Then how do you break the boulders in your garden?

With magic, not metal. Boulders are cleared by the pickaxe spell, which the launch-day walkthrough ties to progressing the prologue and completing Professor Linnea's first music class — the full sequence is covered in [Garden Boulders: Unlocking the Pickaxe Spell](/tasks/unlock-pickaxe-garden-boulders/). The naming overlap is the whole confusion: the spell borrows the tool's name, players search for a pickaxe item, and the item itself leads nowhere.

## Is the pickaxe used in any recipe or quest?

Not one. The game data lists no recipe, combo, or quest use for the pickaxe item, on top of listing no source for it. Compare that with its Tool-category siblings — the [fishing rod](/items/fishing-rod/) is required for a quest line, the [scythe](/items/scythe/) and [bug catcher](/items/bug-catcher/) are daily-use tools — and the pickaxe is clearly the odd one out. Its companion mechanics all live in the [songs overview](/info/songs-overview/) instead.

## Could a future patch make it obtainable?

Possible, but nothing in the current data hints at it. Leftover items like this sometimes get activated in updates, and the version noted here is 1.06 — worth re-checking the [items directory](/items/items-directory/) after any patch. Until then, any guide telling you where to "find" or "buy" a pickaxe item is describing something the game data cannot confirm.
