## Where to Obtain

The launch-day prologue walkthrough lists the cauldron among the first-day school supplies and directs players to pick it up at [Wisk](/locations/wisk/)'s [Newt's Eye Potion Supply](/locations/newts-eye-potion-supply/).

## Version Notes and Issue Reporting

Another troubleshooting resource discusses the lost cauldron and stuck potions class issues in the demo build; this does not imply the same issues exist in the release version, nor does it confirm a universal cauldron recovery command. See [Lost Cauldron or Stuck Potions Class](/tasks/lost-cauldron-potions-class/) for background.
