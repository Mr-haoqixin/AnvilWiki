## Where to buy Flour

| Seller | Availability | Buy price |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| The Mill | All seasons | 20 coins |
| Miller at the Cup & Kettle | Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday | 20 coins |

Pieter can also give Flour during a conversation. Its sell price is **20 coins**.

## How to get Flour from wheat

Process [Wild Wheat](/items/items-ingredients/) at a Wheatmill to make Flour. Flour and a [Watering Can](/items/watering-can/) can then be combined into dough, which is used for baking. The [Flour and Wheatmill quest guide](/tasks/flour-wheatmill-cursed-mill/) covers the mill-related progression.
