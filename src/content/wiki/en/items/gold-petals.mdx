## Uses

The reference-site walkthrough records that crops and Gold Petals buy seeds, gifts, and cosmetics. Shop inventory is limited, the 100,000-petal achievement is a long-term goal, so buy seeds and gifts as needed.

## Selling Items for Currency

For steps to sell items for Gold Petals, see [Sell Items for Gold Petals](/tasks/sell-items-gold-petals/). The 100,000-petal achievement Seasons of Plenty is a long-term goal; for the achievement distribution, see [Achievements Overview](/achievements/achievements-overview/). Gold Petals can also be used to buy song-related items such as [Song of Silence](/items/song-of-silence/).
