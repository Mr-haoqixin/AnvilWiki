## Guarding Bush and Recipe

The walkthrough records that Potion of Hardiness is tied to the Guarding Bush and potion recipes; see [Guarding Bush and Potion of Hardiness](/tasks/guarding-bush-hardiness-potion/). Brewing requires a [Cauldron](/items/cauldron/), and recipe clues are linked to [Dean Clary](/characters/dean-clary/)'s botanical-garden quest line.

## Course Thread

Potion of Hardiness belongs to the Potions 101: Practical Mixes for Practical Witches course thread; the Potions Lab is the related location.
