## Use in the Prologue

The launch-day prologue walkthrough notes that the flute is used to cast magic. Players first talk to [Abigailia](/characters/abigailia/) to receive the letter, then head to [Bristlecone](/characters/bristlecone/)'s shop.

## Pickup Order

The walkthrough explains that Bristlecone first sends players to [Wisk](/locations/wisk/) to gather the other school supplies. Once done, return to his shop to claim the flute. For more route steps, see [First Day School Supplies Walkthrough](/tasks/first-day-school-supplies/).

For limited observations on the flute quiz, see [Flute Quiz: How Crystal and Dew Choices Affect Things](/tasks/flute-quiz-choices/); that page clearly separates demo-build observations from confirmed mechanics.
