## What is the scythe?

Grass regrows all over Glimmerwick, and the scythe is the tool built to keep it in check: the item description in the game files reads "For cutting grass. Uses mana to prevent fatigue." It is one of only five Tool-category items, and the community game-file data (version 1.06) lists no recipe, combo, or quest that consumes it. Its value comes from what cutting grass produces — [Cut Grass and Twigs](/tasks/twigs-cut-grass-scythe/), two of the most-used cheap ingredients in the game.

## Where do you buy the scythe?

[Kavita](/characters/kavita/) sells it for 65 coins in every season, and that is the only acquisition source in the data — no crafting, no foraging, no quest reward. The sellback price of 32 coins is exactly half the buy price, the standard ratio for shop tools. If your garden budget is tight, the [gardening guide](/info/gardening-guide/) can help you decide whether seeds or tools come first.

## What is the scythe used for?

Directly, nothing: the data attaches no recipe or quest to the tool itself. Indirectly, it is the engine behind two ingredient pipelines. Cut Grass feeds the [Potion of Hardiness](/items/potion-of-hardiness/) recipe (2x Cut Grass plus Twigs) and roughly a dozen crafting recipes from trellises to benches, while the grass patches you clear also drop Twigs for the same brew. Cutting with mana instead of stamina means you can clear a whole field without exhausting your witch.

## Does the scythe have any downsides?

The mana cost is the one thing to plan around. Every swing draws from your mana pool — the same resource spell songs use — so long clearing sessions can leave you unable to cast until it refills. Food and rest restore mana, and the fatigue the description mentions only becomes relevant if you cut by hand instead. In practice the trade is strongly in your favor: stamina is harder to restore than mana in the early game.
