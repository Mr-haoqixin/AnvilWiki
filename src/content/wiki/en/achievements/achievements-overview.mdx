## How many achievements does the game have?

The game has 34 Steam achievements, and every unlock condition below is read from game code — cross-checked against community game-file data, version 1.06. Names and descriptions are Steam official English content. Unlock rates (Steam global stats, October 13, 2026) reflect player distribution rather than difficulty: even Enrollment only shows 88.4%, because some buyers have not started yet. Treat the numbers as a real-time map of player distribution, not a difficulty coefficient.

## What are the 12 story achievements?

All twelve unlock through main-story progress, and none of them expire — the developers state that story events do not disappear with the passage of time.

These four are pure calendar gates: each one fires when you reach the first day of the next season, so a missed season simply means waiting for the year to roll around again.

## Which achievements need timing or planning?

The community data attaches no formal missable flags, but the unlock conditions themselves show where the calendar matters. The table below lists every achievement whose window depends on a date, a season, or a one-shot decision.

| Achievement | Timing sensitivity | What to plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spring / Summer / Fall / Winter | Season gate | Each unlocks at the next season's Day 1; nothing is lost by slow play |
| Festival of Plenty | Spring festival | The Seaflower Festival arrives once per year — win the diving contest that day |
| Massive Pumpkin | Fall festival window | Grow the winning pumpkin ahead of the market contest |
| Theo's Gift | Winter festival | Starry Night Gifting comes once per year; the hat is a festival-day outcome |
| Scarf Season | Sheep + shears | Shearing requires shears, whose recipe arrives with the summer camping trip — see [Shears](/items/shears/) |
| Business Buds | Spring crop | Tulips grow from Red Tulip Bulbs; give one to Clover, then talk to her again |
| Spiders for Peony | Tool gate | Five spiders need the bug catcher; it also comes free from a beekeeping quest |
| Into the Sky | Placement one-shot | The colossal pumpkin seed must go into a tree plot, not a normal one |
| Seasons of Plenty | Instant check | 100,000 petals must be held at once — sell nothing big until the moment you cash out |
| Animal Lover | Simultaneous | All four pets must coexist, so do not release or swap one out midway |

## What order should you chase them in?

1. **Enroll first** to unlock fast travel
2. **Invest initial skill points** in gardening
3. **Advance one friendship line at a time**, recommended order Clover→Mel→Florian→Nessa
4. **Attend all classes each season**, win the spring festival then take a breath
5. **Let the year naturally roll to winter**, four seasons completion = game clear
6. **Endgame achievements** (all quests, all songs, all abilities, 100k petals) are second-year projects

## Which achievements can you miss?

None permanently, and that is an explicit design statement: **story events do not expire with the passage of time**, the clock freezes during important events, and the worst case is a seasonal activity waiting until next year. See [Missable Events & Time](/tasks/missable-events-time/) for the full picture.
