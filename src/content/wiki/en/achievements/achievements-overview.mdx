## Achievement Overview

The game has 34 achievements. Launch week (release date September 30, 2026) unlock rates reflect player distribution rather than difficulty: even Enrollment only shows 84%, because some buyers haven't started yet. Treat the numbers as a real-time map of player distribution, not a difficulty coefficient.

## Progression Advice

The reference guide recommends:

1. **Enroll first** to unlock fast travel
2. **Invest initial skill points** in gardening
3. **Advance one friendship line at a time**, recommended order Clover→Mel→Florian→Nessa
4. **Attend all classes each season**, win the spring festival then take a breath
5. **Let the year naturally roll to winter**, four seasons completion = game clear
6. **Endgame achievements** (all quests, all songs, all abilities, 100k petals) are second-year projects
