## Achievement Overview

The game has 34 achievements. Launch week (release date September 30, 2026) unlock rates reflect player distribution rather than difficulty: even Enrollment only shows 84%, because some buyers haven't started yet. Treat the numbers as a real-time map of player distribution, not a difficulty coefficient.

## Progression Advice

The reference guide recommends: enroll first to unlock fast travel, invest initial skill points in gardening, advance one friendship line at a time, attend all classes each season, win the spring festival then take a breath, and let the year naturally roll to winter. Endgame achievements (all quests, all songs, all abilities, 100k petals) are second-year projects.
