## New Features & Convenience Options

- Shop now displays item info cards.
- Added quick transfer button when using controller; the official announcement did not further specify the button's exact operation target.
- Added assist setting to reduce fishing minigame difficulty in options.
- If you own the full game but accidentally launch the demo, the demo now shows a notice to prevent attempting to load full game saves.

## Fixes & Stability

- Corrected description text for Eye of the Wildcrafter ability.
- Fixed difficulty triggering interaction when handing mud pie to Theo.
- Fixed View and Menu key remapping issues.
- Strengthened protection on the "Use right stick to position" tutorial popup to prevent related soft locks.
- Fixed being locked out of Florian's bookshop.
- Fixed soft lock when interacting with NPC while on the way to escape rope teleport.
- Corrected German text errors, and reviewed/modified some English text.

## Version & Source

The official announcement lists the version as Version 1.04b, posted 2026-10-08 21:55 UTC. The official original lists two fixes related to the right stick tutorial; this page merges them into one item; other content follows the announcement's intent.
