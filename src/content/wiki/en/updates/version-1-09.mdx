## What Changed in Version 1.09?

Version 1.09 is primarily a bug-fix update. Several fixes address quest or character availability, while others improve item descriptions and controller behavior.

| Area | Summary |
|-------|---------|
| Quests and characters | Fixed missing classmates in Fall; Zinnia could be absent from [Harp of Six](/tasks/harp-of-six/) if [Fern’s Confrontation](/tasks/ferns-confrontation/) followed the heist; and Nora’s mud-pie delivery option could be missing at the treehouse. |
| Event conflicts | Fixed a conflict between Fern’s Confrontation and the [Seaflower Festival](/locations/seaflower-festival/). |
| Items and delivery | Fixed a flickering container item description, a duplicate Ceaseless Pages reward, and an issue delivering apology worms. |
| Controls | Fixed fishing issues when switching between controller and mouse-and-keyboard input. |
| Other | Added Antoine’s song to the supporter-pack MP3s and corrected most Steam Deck glyphs. |
