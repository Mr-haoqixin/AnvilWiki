## What Changed in Version 1.10?

Version 1.10 focuses on progression blockers and character-related bugs.

| Area | Summary |
|-------|---------|
| Soft locks | Fixed a fishing soft lock involving an unequipped instrument, a soft lock in the [music practice room](/locations/music-class/) with [Azat](/characters/azat/), and a rare Toad Man interaction soft lock. |
| Characters and items | Fixed a case where two Chicories could be obtained and made Citizen 5 non-interactive during the transition to sleep, preventing a soft lock if encountered then. |
| Other | Removed the Steam Deck bug-report button in preparation for Steam Deck verification. |
