## Fixes & Economy Adjustments

- **Arthur** now actually charges 15,000 gold for the flying broom. Before 1.06, the game only checked whether you could afford it, then gave it to you for free.
- Apple chips, coffee, gingerbread, and speed potion are now classified as store items, with sell prices halved. Previously, apple chips bought for 30 gold at Sera's baking stall could be sold back for 30 (or 42 with the Merchant ability).

## Item Price Changes

| Item | Old Price | New Price |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Apple Chips | 30 | **15** |
| Coffee | 35 | **17** |
| Gingerbread | 55 | **27** |
| Potion of Speed | 400 | **200** |
