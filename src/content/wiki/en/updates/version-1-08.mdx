## What's New in Version 1.08?

Version 1.08 is a small follow-up patch: containers now show item info cards, matching the shop cards introduced back in Version 1.04b. Everything else in this patch is a fix, and one of them needs action from affected players.

- **Container info cards** — containers now display item info cards, and the prices on info cards are correct again (see the fix table below).

## Why Did the Camera Pull to the Ocean in the Morning?

A regression made the camera swing toward the ocean when a new morning began, and Version 1.08 fixes it. **Action required: if your save was already affected, you must load your save from the prior night** to get back to a working state.

## What Does Version 1.08 Fix?

| Area | What was fixed |
|-------|----------------|
| Quests and scenes | [Clover](/characters/clover/) no longer goes missing from the fireplace repair scene of [Clover's Calamity](/tasks/clovers-calamity/); no more duplicate Clovers during the finals in the [History Classroom](/locations/history-final/) and during her fishing sequence; the [Green Sprites Lodge](/locations/green-sprites-headquarters/) (HQ) door no longer locks during the restoration chat. |
| Soft locks | Entering the portal before using the key no longer soft-locks the secret meeting room ([The Secret Key](/tasks/the-secret-key/)). |
| Interface | Wrong item prices shown on info cards; trigger portals no longer double-trigger when confirmed with the spacebar. |
| Exploits | The infinite fox exploit from moving the habitat after getting a pet ([fox taming](/tasks/bellian-moor-fox-taming/), [pets overview](/info/pets-overview/)). |
