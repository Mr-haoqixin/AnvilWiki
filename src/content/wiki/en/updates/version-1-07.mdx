## What's New in Version 1.07?

Version 1.07 adds quick transfer for mouse and keyboard, a tutorial modal that also covers stack splitting, and a sort function for the player inventory and containers. Witchfire has quietly been added to a shop's stock as well.

| Feature | How it works |
|---------|--------------|
| Quick transfer | Hold **Shift** and right-click an item to move it between your backpack and an open container. |
| Tutorial modal | A short tutorial window explains quick transfer the first time you use it, including how to split stacks. |
| Sorting | New sort function for the player inventory and for containers. |
| Shop stock | Witchfire can now be bought from a shop; the announcement keeps the details vague to stay spoiler-light. |

For the demo-era keyboard habits around dividing stacks, see [Split Item Stacks: Demo Keyboard Actions](/tasks/split-item-stacks/).

## What Does Version 1.07 Fix?

It fixes more than twenty bugs, ranging from quest soft locks and schedule glitches to economy exploits and world polish. The highlights:

**Quests and scenes**

- Fixed the camera bug on waking during [Codrin](/characters/codrin/)'s quest.
- Fixed being locked inside the dorm while [Clover](/characters/clover/) is sick.
- Fixed the [Celia](/characters/celia/) prank soft lock caused by a conflict with [Acorn](/characters/acorn/)'s quest.
- Fixed [Florian](/characters/florian/)'s schedule reverting after the market day in [Clover's Calamity](/tasks/clovers-calamity/).
- The player character now has barks for the gossip circle when [Mary](/characters/mary/) is at herbotany class.
- [Pieter](/characters/pieter/)'s dialogue now applies the correct time-of-day check for topics about [Nora](/characters/nora/).

**Economy and exploits**

- Fixed being able to skip days by rapid-clicking through the day-end income summary.
- Fixed getting flour for free from the miller in the [cursed mill flour task](/tasks/flour-wheatmill-cursed-mill/).
- Fixed the infinite-money exploit via blueberry seeds.
- Fixed two cases of Clover being duplicated (during finals and a floor-board conflict) and corrected the related stack limits.

**World, items, and controls**

- Item info cards no longer show debug text.
- Quick transfer now rejects non-droppable items.
- Fixed the collision hole near [Mel and Lucina's Shell](/locations/mel-and-lucinas-shell/).
- Improved the protection around escape rope placement — see [Nocturne Forest fast travel ropes](/tasks/nocturne-forest-fast-travel-rope/).
- Critter habitats can now be moved before you acquire a critter ([pets overview](/info/pets-overview/)).
- Fixed music stopping during [apple toss](/locations/apple-toss-minigame/).
- Fixed controller button prompts.
- The in-game bug reporter no longer crashes on long text.

## Does Version 1.07 Change Anything on Steam Deck?

Yes — the UI and text are now larger on Steam Deck, as the game prepares for Steam Deck verification. Current compatibility status and caveats are covered in [Steam Deck Compatibility](/info/steam-deck-compatibility/).
