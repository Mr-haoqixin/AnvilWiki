## Quest, Character & Scene Fixes

- Fixed missing Chinese font glyphs; fixed falling through the world when entering the mole underground.
- Locked the bathhouse during **Nessa**'s wake scene to prevent story conflicts.
- Fixed students following the player after the Headmistress welcome speech, and **Clover** not appearing or appearing twice in the dorm after the winter exam day band performance.
- Fixed **Rowan** missing voice lines, and characters still mentioning flyers after flyer removal.
- Fixed **Mary**'s plant instant-wither scene not fully restoring, and **Fern** leaving when taking the player to **Zinnia** for inquiry.
- Fixed black screen caused by crow delivery on a new day.

## Items, Skills & UI Fixes

- Laid **cobblestone paths** can now be removed with the **Pickaxe**.
- Fixed **Baker's Touch** incorrectly lowering attributes.
- **Munchitis Treatment** can now be dropped and sold.
- Fixed player lockup from rapid double-clicking ability hexes.
- Fixed multilingual text overflow in Ivarice hiding spot letters, and corrected several English typos.
- Temporarily disabled the bug reporter on Steam Deck in preparation for Steam Deck verification.
