## Fixes

- Fixed being able to leave the **summer festival** early, which caused a soft lock.
- Fixed a bug with saving and loading during the **Arthur** broom sequence that caused the avatar to go invisible.
- Fixed the option name for fishing minigame help.
- Fixed the fact that **Arthur** didn't actually take the 15k from you when buying a broom.
- Fixed an infinite money exploit with **Sera**'s shop and **Esteemed Baker**.
