## Fixes & Item Adjustments

- Fixed regression where player-laid **cobble paths** disappeared.
- Increased **Seafloor Sand** spawn frequency.
- Fixed soft locks from random gem and **Amka** high table scene conflicts, and getting stuck from dorm floor creak and fireplace repair conflicts.
- Set mountain bees as non-sellable.
- Removed several deprecated items that the crow friend could gift.

## Camera, UI & Text

- Added option to disable custom NPC camera zoom (during dialogue). Note: character dialogue is designed with camera zoom enabled; disabling it may cause some speakers to be off-screen.
- Fixed new mail tab incorrectly displaying "UI button", and corrected multiple English typos and word choices.
- The announcement still listed other known issues at the time; items not listed here are not necessarily considered unimportant by the developer.
