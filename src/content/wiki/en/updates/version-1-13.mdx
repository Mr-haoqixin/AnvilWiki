## What Changed in Version 1.13?

Version 1.13 fixes several progression and interface issues. One fix is especially useful for players who were already stuck: the developer says loading the latest save should now be enough.

| Area | Summary |
|-------|---------|
| Soft locks and characters | Fixed the [Sea Temple](/locations/sea-temple/) soft lock, including for players already affected; fixed Mel disappearing from her island; and fixed a soft lock caused by moving items quickly at Amka’s stand. |
| Items and containers | Readables can now be purchased even when the inventory is full; fixed a rare case where containers would not close; and clarified the message shown when placing a chest indoors. |
| Calendar and text | Enlarged calendar-event titles, fixed some incorrect Steam Deck glyphs, and corrected font replacement in some non-English readables. |
| Other | Fixed the Brewmaster achievement when changing difficulty mid-playthrough and enabled summoning the boat from the player menu when the boat-summoning amulet is owned. |
