## What Changed in Versions 1.11 and 1.12?

Eastshade Studios published one combined announcement for these two versions. It includes fixes to character schedules, quest scenes, achievements, travel, and localized text.

The temple and achievement fixes are relevant to the [Temple of Silence location page](/locations/temple-of-silence-interior/) and the [achievements overview](/achievements/achievements-overview/).

| Area | Summary |
|-------|---------|
| Characters and events | Fixed characters bunching up east of the [Wisk](/locations/wisk/) entrance bridge and a conflict between [Mel’s](/characters/mel/) bottle and scarf day. **If that event conflict has already affected a save, the developer says to load the save from the previous evening.** |
| Temple of Silence | Fixed a conflict between Gary’s meeting at the [Temple of Silence](/locations/temple-of-silence-interior/) and a music-chamber fix. |
| Achievement | Fixed the [Star Baker achievement](/achievements/achievements-overview/) not registering in certain quick-transfer or direct-drop crafting cases. |
| Travel and inventory | Fixed fast travel being available while swimming and corrected carrot-seed sorting. |
| Localization and controls | Corrected Steam Deck trigger and bumper glyphs, fixed fonts in some readables, and added translations for some strings that previously appeared only in English. |

## Official Source

These notes summarize [Eastshade Studios’ combined Versions 1.11 and 1.12 Steam announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/games/1706510/announcements/detail/1847920302253464), published November 3, 2026.
